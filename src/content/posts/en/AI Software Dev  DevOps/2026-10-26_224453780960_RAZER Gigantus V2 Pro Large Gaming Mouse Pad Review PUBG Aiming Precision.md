Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

​

In addition to handling varied software development and PC work, I regularly enjoy playing FPS games like PUBG (Battleground). When assembling gaming setups, people often invest heavily into mice, mechanical keyboards, and high-refresh monitors, yet treat the mouse pad—the very surface the mouse directly touches and glides over—as an afterthought, relying on cheap desk mats or generic pack-in pads. I previously used generic pads or simple carbon-pattern desk mats myself.

​

Recently, I had the opportunity to try out the newly released high-end gaming mouse pad from gaming gear specialist Razer: the **RAZER GIGANTUS V2 PRO LARGE**, developed incorporating feedback from top esports professionals. Having spent several days using it for daily office tasks and competitive PUBG matches, its premium caliber was immediately palpable. Let's explore its standout features in detail.

​

When the shipping package first arrived, I was surprised by how large the box was for a single mouse pad. Upon opening it, the package was packed securely with substantial bubble wrap cushioning to prevent any denting or creasing of the retail box. Such thoughtful packaging right away speaks volumes about product care.

Examining the underside and border close up: this pad features proprietary **RAZER GLIDECORE™ Foam**. By tuning internal air pocket distribution, it delivers uniform cushioning and tactile consistency during mouse clicks and rapid flicks. The dense textured hexagonal rubber pattern on the base anchors the pad firmly, preventing any unwanted sliding even during intense gaming sessions.

Notice also the **low-profile anti-fray stitched edges**: on standard stitched pads, raised thread edges often scrape against wrists and forearms, causing unpleasant chafing during play. Here, the stitching sits flush with or slightly below the cloth surface, eliminating wrist irritation while preventing edge fraying over time.

According to manufacturer specs, it measures an expansive **500 mm x 480 mm**—a 33% increase in surface area over its predecessor—with a 4 mm thickness. As a reviewer, I couldn't resist verifying this personally using my fishing measuring ruler and digital thickness caliper haha.

​

The width measured exactly 50.0 cm and the height measured 48.0 cm with zero deviation. At 48 cm tall (much deeper than standard 30-40 cm desk mats), it provides a near-square, immense operational area. Caliper measurement showed 3.859 mm, confirming the rated 4 mm spec once minor foam compression is accounted for.

The pad truly shone during **PUBG** matches. In games like PUBG, spotting an opponent demands an instant swipe followed by immediate braking to lock your reticle onto the target and control weapon recoil. Razer claims a control consistency variance of only 3 to 5% between horizontal and vertical swipes, and in practice, pulling down to manage vertical spray felt just as smooth and predictable as horizontal crosshair tracking.

September 1st marks the long-awaited opening of the webfoot octopus season in Korea! I booked a spot aboard **Valkyrie 7-ho** departing from Ocheon Port for opening day.

From early morning, bites were non-stop. Compact egi lures in watermelon and laser pink colors yielded high double-digit counts per hour.

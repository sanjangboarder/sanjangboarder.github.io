On July 11, 2026, I embarked on a summer boat fishing session targeting giant White Croaker (왕백조기) out of Mageompo Port (마검포항) in Taean, Chungnam. Known as one of the premier destinations for trophy-sized croakers, I boarded the popular charter boat **Jayeon Fishing (자연피싱호)** heading toward the famous **Taeseo (태서)** rock island reef point.

### 1. Conditions & Field Action at Taeseo Point

Weather conditions featured light winds under a heatwave warning. However, swells generated by a distant typhoon created murky bottom water conditions at the offshore reefs. 

​

* **Fish Size:** While bites were somewhat delicate, the overall size profile was outstanding. Most fish ranged around 30cm (12 inches), with top-tier trophy catches pushing past 40cm (16 inches).
* **Morning Run:** Filling two baskets during the morning session yielded a solid baseline, with large "King" croakers accounting for roughly 30% of the tally.

### 2. Beat the Heat & Inshore Relocation

As mid-day temperatures soared, staying hydrated and taking breaks inside the ice-cool air-conditioned cabin proved essential. 

​

Later in the afternoon, the captain moved the boat closer inshore to the Mageompo harbor mouth where water clarity improved. Here, I picked up additional steady bites and even landed a nice Yellow Croaker (부세).

#### Why Mageompo Port is Special:
Mageompo features Korea's unique **On-Site Fish Drying Service shop** right at the harbor. For 8,000 KRW per kg, they scale, gut, salt, and semi-dry your croakers, saving you hours of hot, messy cleanup work at home!

​

Furthermore, *Jayeon Fishing* provides descaling mesh nets and coarse sea salt on deck free of charge for anglers who prefer to prep their own catch on the water.

Despite summer heat, the sheer size and pull of Mageompo white croakers made this a memorable outing. Spots on *Jayeon Fishing* fill up weeks in advance, so plan ahead if you want to target these trophy croakers!

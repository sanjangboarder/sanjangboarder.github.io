Hello, this is SanjangBorder.

​

For my third webfoot octopus and cuttlefish outing of the season, I headed down to Hongwon Port to board the New Haeyang Ho. Key ports for webfoot octopus boat fishing in Chungnam Province include Ocheon Port, Daecheon Port, Muchangpo, and Hongwon Port. While Hongwon Port is about a 10 to 20 minute longer drive than other Chungnam harbors, it offers the great advantages of ample parking and productive fishing spots located close to the port.

​

This trip was organized alongside fellow members of TEAM MJT. Everyone arrived early, allowing us to finish departure prep by around 4:30 AM and prepare our gear well before sunrise.

After listening to a brief safety orientation from Captain Atom of the New Haeyang Ho, we set off. It happened to be peak spring tide (Daesari)—the day with the fiercest tidal currents—and the captain was visibly worried. The tide was arguably the worst possible, but with clear skies and calm weather, we departed filled with anticipation.

This season, I continue to rely on ultra-light tackle configurations. Ever since last year, featherweight gear has dominated the webfoot octopus and cuttlefish boat fishing scene. While plenty of effective egi lures exist, my main focus has been sinker selection. Up until a few years ago, the trend was to fish as light as possible, but recently anglers frequently use size 14 even on slack water days, and size 16 or heavier when currents pick up.

​

Because of the roaring spring tide today, I operated primarily with size 14 to 16 sinkers.

The AliExpress rod is astonishingly light with superb sensitivity, but it does carry a slightly fragile feel. It feels like it could snap easily, and with an estimated breaking strength around 2kg, pulling aggressively against severe bottom snags could snap it cleanly in two, requiring care. The Choi Pro rod is an ultra-stiff blank made of 46-ton high-elasticity carbon, so high loading against snags should also be avoided on this rod.

​

In short, ultra-light rods demand careful handling rather than expecting extreme brute durability. In standard fishing conditions lifting webfoot octopus, no major problems should occur.

Personally, in the early season, I have high confidence in E-TEC Omija and Odd Eye egis, or a weighted lead jig (ja) combined with fresh bait and a nude gochujang lure. Since the transit to the first spot took less than 10 minutes, we began fishing in pitch darkness, but as expected, bites were virtually non-existent before daylight.

As the sun crested the horizon, we were treated to a magnificent sunrise. With the morning light, webfoot octopus started latching on bit by bit. Curiously, whether due to the fierce spring tide current or the shallow 5-meter water depth, my usually trusted weighted lead jig rig completely failed to produce today.

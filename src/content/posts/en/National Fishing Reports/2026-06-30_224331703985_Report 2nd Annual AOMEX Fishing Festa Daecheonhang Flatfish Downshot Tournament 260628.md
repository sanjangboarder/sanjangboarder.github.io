On June 28, 2026, I participated in the **2nd Annual AOMEX Fishing Festa Flatfish Downshot Tournament** out of Daecheonhang Port (대천항). Over 100 competitive boat anglers gathered for this event, and I was assigned to the famous *Superman-ho* of the Team Luffy Fleet.

AOMEX and Team Luffy Fleet went above and beyond to ensure anglers were well taken care of from dawn to dusk:

* **Coffee Truck at Ceremony:** Unlimited fresh beverages provided after returning to harbor.

### 2. Field Action & Tackle Testing

For this tournament, I tested the new **AOMEX Navigator Flatfish Downshot Rod**. Featuring a responsive 8:2 fast tip, the rod offered excellent sensitivity and backbone.

​

#### The Action
While target flatfish were somewhat finicky, I managed to land a chunky Greenling (방망이 놀래미) and a juvenile Rockfish. Unfortunately, flatfish eluded me for the day—though an angler standing just two spots down from me landed a tournament 3rd-place flatfish!

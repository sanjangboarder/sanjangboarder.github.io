### 1. Spot Profile & Atmosphere

The Mokpo New Port pier area offers paved, secure footing and easy parking, making it a favorite fishing haven for local families.

​

* **Footing:** Wide concrete piers with excellent safety for night fishing.
* **Tackle Strategy:** Because early August cutlassfish average 1.3 to 1.5 finger-widths (1.5지), using an ultra-light spinning rod paired with micro jigheads is essential to avoid short-bites.

​

---

### 2. Tackle & Rigging Setup

* **Rod:** Banax Moonlight 732ULS (Ultra-Light Solid Tip).
* **Reel:** Shimano Vanquish C2500S.
* **Main Line:** #0.8 PE Braided Line.
* **Jighead:** Baekkyung 2g Reverse Jighead (역지그). *(Note: Heavier 5g+ jigheads sank too rapidly for early-season fish).*
* **Lure:** Berkley Gulp! Sandworm 2-inch.
* **Light:** 153 Craft 180W Gathering Light.

### 3. Tactical Action & Catch Results

Fishing during the last 2 hours of the incoming tide yielded steady strikes along the shadow boundary of the high-powered gathering light.

​

* **Retrieve Pattern:** 2 quick rod jerks followed by a 3-second tensioned fall. Over 70% of strikes occurred right as the lure hovered along the light-shadow boundary.
* **Results:** Landed 7 juvenile cutlassfish (1.3 to 1.5 finger-width). All fish were safely released back into Mokpo Harbor.

### Conclusion

While size remains small in early August, Mokpo New Port provides a comfortable, accessible night fishing spot. Downsizing to a 2g micro jighead and 2-inch sandworm proved to be the secret for securing bites.

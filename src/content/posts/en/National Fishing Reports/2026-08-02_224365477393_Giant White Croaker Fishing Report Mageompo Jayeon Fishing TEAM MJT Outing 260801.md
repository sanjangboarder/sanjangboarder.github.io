### 1. Bait Preparation & Rigging Tips

* **Lugworm Bait:** Lugworms (청개비) are available at the harbor tackle shop for 10,000 KRW per tub.
* **Pro Baiting Tip:** For standard croakers, trimming worms to cover the hook bend works well. However, when targeting **trophy 30–40cm+ King Croakers**, threading a **whole uncut lugworm** significantly increases your chances of triggering larger fish!
* **Quantity:** Bring at least 2 tubs per angler for a full-day session.

#### Heatstroke Prevention Warning
By mid-day, heatwave warnings went into effect. Staying out on open decks under intense sun can easily trigger heat exhaustion. Whenever feeling lightheaded, taking 15-minute cooling breaks inside *Jayeon Fishing's* air-conditioned cabin is crucial for safety.

### 1. Interior & Ordering System

The restaurant is operated as a solo chef kitchen using a self-service kiosk ordering system. The interior is neat with traditional Japanese cozy wall decorations.

​

* **Menu Lineup:** Tonkotsu Ramen, Miso Ramen, Spicy Ramen, and Butadon (Japanese Pork Rice Bowl).
* **Set Options:** Combination sets including a main ramen bowl plus a mini Butadon.

### 2. Tonkotsu Ramen Assessment

I ordered the Tonkotsu Ramen Set with an extra Chashu pork topping (2,000 KRW for 4 slices).

​

* **Broth & Noodles:** The pork bone broth is clean but somewhat predictable, lacking the deep rich collagen signature found in specialized artisanal ramen houses.
* **Chashu Quality:** Slices vary slightly in thickness; while thinner cuts are tender, thicker cuts tend to be slightly dry.

### 3. The Highlight: Smoky Pork Rice Bowl (Butadon)

The mini **Butadon (부타동)** served in the set turned out to be the true highlight of the meal!

​

* **Flavor Profile:** Tender sliced pork glazed with sweet-savory tare sauce and seared to produce a delicious smoky char aroma.
* **Verdict:** The rice bowl offers far more flavor harmony than the ramen broth. Ordering a full-size Butadon bowl is highly recommended.

### Personal Verdict

* **Rating:** 3.3 / 5.0
* **Final Thoughts:** While the ramen is standard casual fare, the smoky Butadon pork rice bowl makes Majikado a decent local lunch option when visiting Songdo Moonlight Park.

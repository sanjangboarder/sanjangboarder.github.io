Today I am sharing an honest personal dining review of **Sodam Jjamppong Soft Tofu (소담 짬뽕순두부)**, located along Daebuhwanggeum-ro in Daebudo Island, Ansan. While Daebudo is famous for clam knife-cut noodles (kalguksu), this spot offers spicy seafood soft tofu stew (jjamppong sundubu).

### 1. Interior & Menu Options

Visiting around 11:30 AM on a Saturday, the dining hall is spacious with plenty of seating suitable for tourists.

​

* **Main Menu:** Jjamppong Soft Tofu Stew (requires minimum 2-person order).
* **Side Options:** Seafood Scallion Pancake (해물파전) and Small Octopus Dumplings (낙지만두).

### 2. Side Dishes: Seafood Scallion Pancake & Octopus Dumplings

​

#### Seafood Scallion Pancake (20,000 KRW)
Unfortunately, the pancake was disappointing for its price. It lacked crispiness, and the seafood portion was surprisingly sparse compared to other local Daebudo spots.

​

#### Octopus Dumplings (낙지만두)
The octopus dumplings fared much better. Featuring a chewy translucent skin stuffed with minced octopus and meat filling, they provided a satisfying texture.

### 3. Main Dish: Jjamppong Soft Tofu Stew

The main stew is served in a tabletop hotpot over a gas burner. 

​

* **Ingredients:** Fresh soft tofu, two live abalones, clams, mussels, and spicy jjamppong broth.
* **Taste:** The seafood broth is decent, but the ratio of soup to soft tofu felt slightly sparse.

### Personal Verdict

* **Rating:** 3.3 / 5.0
* **Final Thoughts:** While not a destination restaurant, if you are visiting Daebudo and want a spicy alternative to standard kalguksu noodle soup, Sodam Jjamppong Soft Tofu is an okay casual option.

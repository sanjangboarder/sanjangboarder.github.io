### 1. Dining Hall & Menu Options

Visiting for Friday brunch right as doors opened, the spacious dining room filled up quickly with tourists and travelers heading toward the Saemangeum area.

​

* **Main Menu:** Assorted Grilled Fish Set (모듬생선구이) at 17,000 KRW per person (requires minimum 2-person order).
* **Side Dishes:** Served with soy-marinated crab, seasoned cockles, blowfish skin salad, squid, salted hairtail entrail dip, and soybean paste soup.

### 2. Main Dish Evaluation: Assorted Grilled Fish Platter

The grilled fish platter arrives piping hot, pan-fried to a golden crisp.

​

1. **Grilled Bakdae (박대 / Tongue Sole) — BEST:** Gunsan's local delicacy took 1st place. Perfectly salted with a crispy exterior and tender, white meat that de-bones effortlessly.
2. **Grilled Sea Robin (성대) — 2nd Place:** Plump, firm meat texture with a clean, mild flavor.
3. **Mackerel & Flounder (고등어 / 가자미):** Properly cooked, though freshness was somewhat average compared to the fresh Bakdae.

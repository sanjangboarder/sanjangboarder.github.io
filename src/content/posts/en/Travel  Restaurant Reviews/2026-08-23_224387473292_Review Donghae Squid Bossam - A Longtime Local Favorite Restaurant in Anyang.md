Today, I am sharing a review of a restaurant in Anyang Ilbeonga that I have been visiting regularly for over 20 years: **Donghae Squid Bossam (동해 오징어보쌈)**. It is a long-standing local favorite spot that virtually anyone living in Anyang knows about.

​

On the menu, **Squid Bossam (오징어보쌈)** is the main flagship item, and ordering the **Seokkeo-jjigae (섞어찌개 / Mixed Squid Stew)** alongside it is practically considered an unwritten rule here.

​

Please note that menu items require a minimum order of 2 servings. Even if you visit alone, you must order 2 servings... Additions are also ordered in 2-serving minimums... But the portion size feels quite generous, and there is a clear reason for that.

Individual bowls of cold radish water kimchi (Dongchimi) are served for each person. That makes it 5 side dishes total, and all these side dishes share one key common characteristic:

**Every single side dish is specifically designed to neutralize spicy heat.**

The main Squid Bossam dish at Donghae is genuinely spicy. Because it is deliciously spicy, you end up eating huge amounts of these side dishes to neutralize the heat. In particular, this Dongchimi cold radish soup is indispensable, haha.

You can eat it straight, but wrapping it in lettuce wraps is strongly recommended. Layering the provided side dishes one by one creates a fantastic flavor combination. Adding bean sprouts or pickled scallion bulbs (Rakkyo) works great... And if you order rice, adding a spoonful of rice to the wrap is ideal.

The reason I didn't order rice on this day was because when ordering, I specifically requested the owner to **make it less spicy**, haha.

If you plan to eat the original spice level, ordering rice will be absolutely essential to maintain peace in your mouth.

​

Because the original Donghae Squid Bossam is very spicy, side dishes are consumed in large quantities. That is why they operate a self-service refill bar where you can fetch extra side dishes as desired... Because you eat side dishes along with it, a 2-person order is more than enough to leave you completely full.

---

​

I mentioned that Donghae Squid Bossam in Anyang is spicy, but it is genuinely delicious. As I get older, I now ask for it to be made less spicy, but even with that request, people sensitive to spice might still find it overwhelming. Unless you love spicy food, asking for a less spicy level is strongly recommended.

​

On my personal restaurant rating scale, I rate this place **4.5 out of 5.0**. It is a restaurant I keep returning to whenever I crave it, and even after 20 years, the taste remains unchanged with nostalgic memories. Although it is a 40-minute drive from home, I visit 2 to 3 times a year. If you love spicy Korean food and are seeking a unique flavor experience, visiting here will leave zero regrets.

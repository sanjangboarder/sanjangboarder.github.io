Finding a reliable early morning breakfast restaurant before a fishing trip can be tricky. Today I review **Yuksudang**, located in Songdo.

Opening early in the morning, Yuksudang serves deep, savory Korean beef bone soups (*Gukbap*), including Sun-dae Gukbap and Beef Rice Soup.

​

​

The side dishes, including seasoned kkakdugi kimchi, pair perfectly with the hot broth. It is an ideal breakfast stop before long drives to the port.

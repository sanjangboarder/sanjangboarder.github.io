Craving pasta, I took my wife and kids to a pasta place located behind the Songdo Coast Guard Headquarters. The restaurant's name is **Sahwal Pasta & Brunch Cafe**. Please refer to the map below for its location and operating hours.

<div class="map-card glass"><div class="map-info"><span class="map-icon">📍</span><div class="map-text"><div class="map-name">Sahwal Pasta & Brunch Cafe</div><div class="map-addr">Rooms 201-202, 2F, Building A, Eunha Plaza, 19 Harmony-ro 36beon-gil, Yeonsu-gu, Incheon</div></div></div><a href="https://map.naver.com/p/search/사생활+파스타&브런치카페" target="\_blank" class="map-btn">View Map</a></div>

Parking is available in the building's basement, but there are only 6 spaces, and driving in and out is quite tricky. It should be fine for compact cars up to the Avante (Elantra) size, but if you drive an SUV or large sedan like a Santa Fe or Grandeur, you will face quite a few headaches when going down or coming up the parking ramp. If you are very confident in your driving skills, you can bring your car, but if you're hesitant, it would be much wiser to park elsewhere.

​

The restaurant is on the 2nd floor, and you can also make reservations directly via Naver Maps.

Since it is located on the second floor of a small commercial building, I honestly did not have very high expectations before entering. However, once inside, I was pleasantly surprised. You immediately get a strong sense that they put a lot of genuine thought and effort into the interior decor.

We ordered one Sanchon Steak, the Assorted Katsu Platter, and two varieties of pasta.

The basic assorted pickles served: when I searched online, I found that "Sahwal" is actually a franchise brand with about 10 branch locations. I had never encountered the brand before, so I had no idea haha. If I hadn't known it was a franchise, I would have assumed the pickles were house-made in the kitchen. They have a pleasant, universally appealing flavor that anyone would enjoy at a casual restaurant.

The first dish served was the Arrabiata Tomato Pasta. It was priced around 17,000 KRW and came with several generously sized shrimp. You certainly don't get the feeling that they skimped on ingredients at all.


<div class="single-image">
<img src="https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMjJfNjIg/MDAxNzkyNjQ4MTgxMjg3.PnYAH_rS3Xgq1U1xRA5AIl-tmLuwY4u29LVosZRWvrgg.4vyTP5O8y3K7fjwsfH3ojAv1cwu_E1-dyVRRMdcCd8og.JPEG/900_20261021_190238.jpg?type=w800" />

Compared to other Arrabiata tomato pastas I've had elsewhere, this version seemed to emphasize a sharper kick of spice. It's a reliable dish that very few people would find fault with.

Next came the Country Oil Pasta (Sigol Oil Pasta). The price was identical. Looking at the menu, I initially wondered if it featured arugula, but it actually prominently features perilla leaves (kkaennip) and garlic, loaded generously with thinly sliced pork belly (daepae samgyeopsal).


<div class="single-image">
<img src="https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMjJfMjUy/MDAxNzkyNjQ4MTg2Njcy.BYgLhH0FzqYRf3cdeymI_STlqtTThQdQXNYwgmc2NK8g.c-TUHIRc-CuI3hNG-YJjk9hYkuJKAqv9pWk5lnk_0K4g.JPEG/900_20261021_190235.jpg?type=w800" />

And while I didn't expect it to be very spicy, this turned out to be the spiciest dish among everything we ordered that evening. Usually, pasta spice comes from familiar dried peperoncino peppers, but here it had a sharp, pungent bite reminiscent of Cheongyang chili peppers or concentrated capsaicin. Young kids will probably struggle to eat this, making it better suited for adults who actively enjoy serious spice.

Now onto the house signature dish: the Sanchon Steak. I believe the price was around 29,000 KRW, which offers quite reasonable value for money as steaks go, and there are several unique touches... namely, bean sprouts steeped in gochujang chili paste sauce, paired with a rather unorthodox plating style.

They stated that the beef is cooked sous-vide, and it did indeed have a tender texture. Looking at the photo I took now, however, it does feel slightly less substantial than what was pictured on the menu.

All in all, both of my sons and my wife enjoyed the meal, while my own satisfaction was about halfway. If I were to rate it by my personal standards, I would give it around 3.7 out of 5 stars. That being said, the spatial layout is extremely convenient for year-end gatherings, making me want to consider it for dining with acquaintances. My primary concern remains the parking situation: there are numerous other commercial parking garages nearby, so parking there and walking over wouldn't be an issue, but explaining the parking directions to guests is a bit complicated and inconvenient.

​

Since this venue originally identifies as a brunch cafe and we visited during dinner hours, I will likely return at least once more specifically to sample their daytime brunch menu.

Thank you.

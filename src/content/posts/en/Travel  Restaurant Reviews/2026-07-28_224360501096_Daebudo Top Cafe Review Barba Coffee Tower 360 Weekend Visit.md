The 1st-floor bakery offers an impressive variety of fresh pastries, artisan breads, and desserts. While pricing is on the higher end for mega-cafes, the quality and taste live up to expectations.

The revolving tower floor slowly completes a full 360-degree rotation over 60 minutes. From the window seats, guests enjoy panoramic views of Sihwa Lake, Songdo skyline, Yeongheungdo, and distant coastal islands.

Highlight recommendation: The local **Daebudo Grape Soft Ice Cream** is a delicious must-try dessert!

​

Overall Rating: 4.3 / 5.0. It is a fantastic destination for couples and family weekend drives.

Today I am sharing a personal dining review of **Ddungbo-jib (뚱보집)** located in the 8th District of Songdo, Incheon. Famous for its thick pre-seared grilled pork rinds (돼지껍데기) and savory specialty pork cuts, it is a local neighborhood go-to spot for pork enthusiasts.

### 1. Table Setting & Side Dishes

The banchan (side dish) lineup keeps things straightforward so the pork takes center stage:

​

* **Egg-Yolk Scallion Salad:** Freshly sliced green scallions topped with a raw egg yolk for extra richness.
* **Mel-Jeot Sauce:** Anchovy dip simmered on the grill with sliced garlic and spicy Cheongyang peppers.
* **Sides:** Pickled radish, kimchi, and pickled garlic leaves (myeong-i namul).

### 2. Large Specialty Platter (모듬구이)

I ordered the Large Pork Platter, featuring pork belly (삼겹살), five-layer pork (오겹살), pork jowl (항정살), and fatty specialty cuts.

​

* **Texture & Flavor:** The cuts lean toward higher fat distribution, giving them a juicy, chewy mouthfeel that pairs perfectly with the simmering Mel-jeot sauce.
* **Side Pairing:** Instead of standard naengmyeon noodles, pairing grilled pork with their spicy cold Bibim-myeon noodles delivers an incredible combination!

### 3. The Highlight: Thick Pre-Seared Pork Rind (돼지껍데기)

The real star of Ddungbo-jib is their signature **Grilled Pork Rind**:

​

* **Pre-Seared Thickness:** Unlike thin, chewy pork skins at typical BBQ joints, Ddungbo-jib serves thick cuts pre-seared with grid grill marks.
* **Texture:** Tender, pillowy, and rich rather than rubbery. Dipping hot grid-marked pork rind into roasted soybean powder (콩가루) creates an addictive savory-nutty combination.

Today I am sharing a food review of **Ojangdong Heungnambjib**, a legendary Seoul restaurant famous for authentic Hamheung-style cold noodles (*Naengmyeon*).

​

​

Operating since 1953, Heungnambjib is renowned for its chewy sweet potato starch noodles and spicy seasoned skate/flounder raw fish topping (*Hoe-Naengmyeon*).

​

<img src="https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNTA5MDFfODg4/MDAxNzU2NzE4ODg4ODg4.a1s2d3f4g5.JPEG/900_20250830_123520.jpg?type=w800" />

​

The warm beef broth (*Yuksu*) served before the meal is rich and savory. Mixing the spicy raw fish noodles with a dash of sesame oil and vinegar delivers unforgettable flavor.

​

<img src="https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNTA5MDFfNjY2/MDAxNzU6NzE4NjY2NjY2.z9x8c7v6b5.JPEG/900_20250830_125000.jpg?type=w800" />
</div>

​

If you appreciate traditional Korean cold noodles, a visit to Ojangdong Heungnambjib is an absolute must.

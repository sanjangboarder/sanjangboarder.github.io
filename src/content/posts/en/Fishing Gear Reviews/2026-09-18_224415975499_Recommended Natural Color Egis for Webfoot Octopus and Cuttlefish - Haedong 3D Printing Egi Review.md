Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

When the webfoot octopus and cuttlefish season arrives, I introduce various egi lures to you. This time, I am posting about a distinctive offering from Haedong: the 3D Printing Egi.

The product name itself is the 3D Transfer Print Egi. Among countless egi lure designs, this product stands out because it is split into two internal construction types—internal glow plate and internal silver reflective plate—and the lineup consists exclusively of natural colorways.

Consequently, rather than descriptive color names, they are simply categorized by numbers from #1 through #10. Models #1 through #5 feature an internal glow plate, while #6 through #10 house an internal silver plate.

Let me show you the models with internal glow plates first in sequence. Lures equipped with glow plates offer great merit before sunrise or in deep water situations exceeding 30 meters where ambient sunlight rarely penetrates. Because all Haedong 3D Transfer Print egis fall under natural color profiles, they show their greatest prowess on days with muddy water clarity or when webfoot octopus and cuttlefish activity drops.

To compare the glow and Keimura UV effects between the glow plate and silver plate in greater detail: in the photo below, the top lure has the silver plate, and the bottom lure has the glow plate. The luminescent glow is clearly superior on the glow plate model, exactly as intended. While Keimura reaction is present on the glow plate too, it appears distinctly more pronounced on the silver plate model.

These days, webfoot octopus and cuttlefish encounter so many high-end premium egis that anglers need to factor in rotations between when glow works versus when it doesn't, and when intense Keimura outperforms subtle tones. Having these two types in your tackle box makes covering diverse underwater situations vastly easier.

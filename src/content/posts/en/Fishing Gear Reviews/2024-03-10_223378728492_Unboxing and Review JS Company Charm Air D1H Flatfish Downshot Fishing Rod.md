My very first fishing rod purchase for the 2024 season is the **JS Company Charm Air D1H**. This model features a fast 8:2 tip action designed specifically for flatfish downshot boat fishing. Taking advantage of a 20% discount coupon from Younsung, I acquired it for approximately 245,000 KRW (down from its retail price of around 300,000 KRW).

​

Having previously used the 2020 Charm Air D1H model, I was eager to inspect the updated 2024 version and evaluate its technical improvements.

​

---

**Key Specifications & Features**

* **Blank:** High-modulus 40-Ton Carbon Fiber construction.
* **Target Application:** Saltwater boat fishing flatfish downshot and bottom jigging.
* **Guides:** Fuji Titanium Frame SiC Guide System.
* **Weight:** 95g (Ultra-lightweight class).
* **Length:** 1.9m overall length with a 2-piece offset joint design.

**Bend Test & Load Analysis**

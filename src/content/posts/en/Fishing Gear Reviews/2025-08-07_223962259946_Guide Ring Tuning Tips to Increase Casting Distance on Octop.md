Stock guides on budget octopus rods often feature small inner diameters or rough ceramic rings that create line friction during long casts.

​

​

Upgrading stripper and tip guides to Fuji SiC or Alconite rings reduces line slap, adding 5 to 10 meters to your casting distance when fishing from shore breakwaters.

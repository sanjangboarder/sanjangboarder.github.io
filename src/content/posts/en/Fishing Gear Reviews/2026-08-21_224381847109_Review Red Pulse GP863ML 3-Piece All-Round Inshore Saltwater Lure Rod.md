### 1. Weight Breakdown & Rear Grip Extension

* **Section Weight Measurements:** Tip section 1 (8.2g) + Mid section 2 (20.8g) + Butt section 3 (83.3g).
* **Total Weight:** **112g** with rear grip extension (106.5g without extension cap).
* **Rear Extension Cap:** A removable 5.4g rear carbon extension lets anglers adjust rear grip length between 32cm and 36cm based on casting preference.

### 2. Physical Dimensions & Reel Seat Balance

* **Packability:** Each 3-piece segment measures under 90cm, fitting easily into compact 1-meter rod travel cases.
* **Diameters:** Tip diameter: 1.53mm / Mid diameter: 5.86mm / Butt diameter: 11.85mm / Carbon rear grip: 22.16mm.
* **Balance:** Paired with a Shimano Sephia C3000SDH spinning reel, the balance point leans slightly forward of the reel seat foregrip.

### Conclusion

While the Red Pulse GP863ML offers solid high-modulus carbon stiffness and 3-piece portability, its balance and total weight (112g) fall slightly short of the legendary feel of older Red Pulse XP series rods. Nevertheless, it remains a stiff, crisp travel option for shore casting anglers.

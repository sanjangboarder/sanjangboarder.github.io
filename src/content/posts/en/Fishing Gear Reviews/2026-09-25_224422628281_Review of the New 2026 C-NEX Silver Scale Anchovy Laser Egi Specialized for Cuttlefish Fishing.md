Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

When I rotate egis during webfoot octopus or cuttlefish fishing trips, lures that never fail to make my starting lineup are C-NEX's Chokkomi egi and Silver Scale Anchovy (Eunbineul Myeolchi) egi. Through the September webfoot octopus and cuttlefish season, I mostly deploy the Chokkomi egi, and from October onwards when the sizes get bigger for cuttlefish and octopus, I add the Silver Scale Anchovy egi into my rotation.

For 2026 as well, C-NEX has released a brand-new version of the Silver Scale Anchovy egi, so I quickly got my hands on them to introduce them to you.

C-NEX shipped them in secure packaging so that I could showcase the entire lineup of the Silver Scale Anchovy egi series to date.

The Silver Scale Anchovy egis released each year differ in color schemes. The new 2026 release adds brand-new colors that did not exist previously.

While the 2024 version stood out with natural color tones and pinpoint luminous glow effects, the 2025 Silver Scale Anchovy egi added popular colors like watermelon and green, with an increased emphasis on Keimura (UV) glow.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjRfMTY2/MDAxNzkwMjEyMjEyMTQ5.fj07kFGNezo7wwq56PHtL1yHnCfD1st1pUBZ8Y7YLhwg.dYiRRhfedpqhQfLD5zJIs8qqPn13RUVSC47Wli2XloQg.JPEG/SE-f57cee9c-b7a8-11f1-8444-8ff28b06d989.jpg?type=w800)

A total of 5 colors have been newly introduced. They include the ever-popular Gochujang (red head) and Watermelon laser, one modeled after Mackerel, and two additional natural-tone colors. The main difference from previous Silver Scale Anchovy lures is highlighted in the product name itself: **Laser** is appended to Silver Scale Anchovy, featuring an eye-catching laser reflection effect along the lateral sides of the body.

First, let's take a closer look at the Gochujang (red head) color to examine its detailed appearance. As expected from C-NEX, a domestic fishing tackle manufacturer known for high build quality with its own production facilities in Korea, the finish and construction of each component on this Anchovy egi are truly superb, just like their previous products.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjRfMTUx/MDAxNzkwMjEyMjkxMTQx.7hOfCtcPjBaC_8d_sSR0PDyZdfPmEld7gKl6AQvkKvwg.qnxNYjxo4Ja1HwHiGG5nferzOeyeHVL3lLsV4TnFENMg.JPEG/SE-f57f86b2-b7a8-11f1-8444-a7544ddb1a65.jpg?type=w800)

The biggest difference from the existing Silver Scale Anchovy is that an irregular laser effect pattern is applied across the body. When viewed from above or at a quarter angle, it looks like a standard Gochujang red head, but from the profile side, it exhibits a full laser holographic reflection. You can consider it as the classic fish scale pattern of the 2024 and 2025 Silver Scale Anchovy having evolved into a laser holographic pattern.

The belly section also features a luminous glow effect. When the egi is falling through the water column, the glowing belly captures the attention of cuttlefish and webfoot octopuses from below. Once it hits bottom and stays in a horizontal swimming posture, the side profile emits intense laser reflections for dual-action appeal, which is very impressive.

The Silver Scale Anchovy Laser egi shares the same specifications as the previous series. Measuring from the nose swivel to the double hooks, it is about 10 cm long, with a body-only length of around 7.5 cm. It weighs 8.1 g, which is not overly heavy considering its overall length. The lure itself is configured for slow floating by default, and when paired with a size 8 to 10 snap swivel at the nose, it transitions into slow sinking, making it perfectly optimized as a cuttlefish egi.

The recent trend in egis is to differentiate phosphorescent glow and Keimura UV glow across color variations so anglers can adapt to various water conditions. The Silver Scale Anchovy Laser egi features luminous glow across its eyes, hook base, head, and broadly along the belly. You can consider phosphorescent glow to be built into all models by default.

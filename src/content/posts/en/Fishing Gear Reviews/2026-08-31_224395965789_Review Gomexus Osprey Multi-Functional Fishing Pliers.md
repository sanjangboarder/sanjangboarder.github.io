### 1. 316 Marine Stainless & Titanium Replaceable Cutters

The rotating circular joint adopts **316 Marine Grade Stainless Steel**, while the line cutter blades and bolts are constructed with **Titanium**. These corrosion-resistant materials prevent saltwater rust even under harsh ocean environments. Simply rinsing them with fresh water after trips keeps them rust-free for years.

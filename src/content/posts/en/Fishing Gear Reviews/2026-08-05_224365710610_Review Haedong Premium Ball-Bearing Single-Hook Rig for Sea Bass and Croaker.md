### 1. Why Single-Hook Rigs (외수질 채비)?

While double-hook rigs allow catching two fish at once during active feeding frenzies, single-hook dropper rigs offer distinct advantages when fishing is slow:

​

* **Natural Live Bait Presentation:** Allows live prawns or sandworms to flutter naturally with zero line resistance.
* **Tangle-Free Design:** Positioning the branch swivel closer to the sinker prevents the dropper line from wrapping around the main leader during rapid descents.

### 2. Technical Specifications & Ball-Bearing Hardware

* **Main Rig Leader:** 120cm #18 Nylon Monofilament (heavy abrasion resistance against rocks and monster Seabass).
* **Branch Line Recommendation:** Pair with a #8 Nylon branch line so snags break only the hook section, saving the main rig.
* **Premium Swivels:** Features glow-in-the-dark rolling sleeve swivels and high-spin ball-bearing snap swivels at both ends to eliminate line twist under heavy currents.

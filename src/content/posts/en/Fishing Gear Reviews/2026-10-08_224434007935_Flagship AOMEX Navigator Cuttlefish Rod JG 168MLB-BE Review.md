Although I am still far from being at the top-tier master level, I always feel eager to try out and learn from the tackle setups used by seasoned angling experts. The AOMEX Navigator Cuttlefish Rod is engineered as a 2-piece butt-joint structure. In terms of storage compactness, a center-cut 2-piece would have a shorter packed length, but a butt joint is unquestionably the best architecture to maximize sensitivity and preserve pure blank performance while maintaining practical portability.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMDVfMjg4/MDAxNzkxMTk1MDQyMDA4.YI0WgpK8izT0vVCVFJ6o10P6nyNx3XbZZVDWw_0uD10g.-RHusLuIpcVY8ikMTK23erAbwZXS39axAzoTAbzJWNUg.JPEG/SE-a8bf5b12-bf83-11f1-9454-d183177a40cf.jpg?type=w800)

There are 13 guides positioned strategically to distribute blank load smoothly across the entire bend curve. The top four tip guides feature high-visibility accent wraps to ensure crisp visual tracking of the rod tip. The inner guide diameters hit the sweet spot—enhancing tactile sensitivity while enabling smooth, frictionless line flow during casting. As a truly high-end rod, the AOMEX Navigator cuttlefish rod is equipped with genuine FUJI Titanium frames and premium SiC rings for outstanding reliability.

The tip section utilizes what the manufacturer calls a "Pureless Tip," where eco-friendly materials are processed and layered via high-tech methods into a carbon-like structure, transmitting extremely natural, crystal-clear feedback to the angler.

Observing the center of gravity, it sits directly inside the reel seat rather than forward in front of it, indicating impeccable rod balance. Even if a rod has a slight physical weight, having a centered balance point creates an effortless, agile, and feather-light sensation in hand during actual fishing.

The rod taper is officially categorized as a 7:3 action. From my experience across two actual boat outings, with a size 20 sinker it feels around 7.5:2.5, whereas with lighter sinkers it exhibits an 8:2 feel. In other words, while it leans toward moderate-fast suppleness, the ultra-high modulus Toray T1100 and M40X blanks endow it with rapid recovery and crisp rigidity.

Personally, I used to consider softer actions somewhat disadvantageous for tactile weight-sensing techniques while excelling at visual rod-tip reading. However, the Navigator manages both exceptionally: it conveys clear tactile weight differences while its tip visually exposes the subtlest changes.

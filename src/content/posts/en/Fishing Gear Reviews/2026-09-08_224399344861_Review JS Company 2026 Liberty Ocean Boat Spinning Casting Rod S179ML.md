Digital caliper measurements showed a **tip diameter of 1.631 mm** and a **butt diameter of 10.472 mm**. The tip diameter matches general inshore rods while the butt diameter is slightly slimmer. Thanks to its shorter 1.8m blank length, it provides a crisp, medium-stiff recovery feel with zero blank wobbling.

### 2. Balance Point & Reel Pairing

Given the short rear grip and rearward reel seat placement, I initially wondered if it would suffer from tip-heaviness. Surprisingly, balance was actually superior to typical long inshore rods!

​

### Conclusion

Using long 8ft to 9ft shore inshore rods on crowded charter decks creates constant safety hazards. The Liberty Ocean S179ML solves this by offering a compact 1.8m profile that allows safe, comfortable casting in tight boat spaces. For anglers interested in boat flatfish casting or night rockfish lure fishing, this rod is practically an unrivaled choice.

**Key Rigging Upgrades:**
1. **Wire Leader Length:** Keep wire leaders to 7cm–10cm. Longer wire dampens lure darting action.
2. **Glow Bead Position:** Place a single UV luminescent bead 2cm above the jighead eye to draw strikes toward the hook rather than main line.
3. **Stinger Hooks:** Add a small size #10 stinger treble hook to soft plastic tails when fish are biting short.

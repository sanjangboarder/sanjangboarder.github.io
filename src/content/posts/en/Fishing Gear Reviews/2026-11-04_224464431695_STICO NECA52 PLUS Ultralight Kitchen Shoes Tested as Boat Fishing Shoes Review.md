Normally, professionals working in food service or commercial kitchens prioritize safe kitchen footwear above all else. In my case, because I frequently head out to sea for boat fishing on weekends, I have an immense interest in shoes that never slip on wet surfaces and keep feet comfortable through full days of standing haha.

​

The maritime deck environment on a boat is just as demanding as a commercial kitchen floor—in fact, arguably even harsher. Seawater constantly washes over the deck, and when rain falls, boat flooring becomes as slick as a frozen ice rink. Furthermore, during autumn cuttlefish season, jet-black viscous ink splatters profusely across the deck; wearing ordinary sneakers or standard casual foam clogs is practically an invitation to slip and fall.

​

For these reasons, I decided to test the STICO NEC-A52 PLUS Comfort Kitchen Shoes—a hybrid work clog from STICO, renowned for their globally patented slip-resistance technology, that combines workplace safety with sleek casual styling—and evaluate them directly from home to the actual offshore fishing decks.

Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Most people who go fishing travel by car... In fact, it is probably well over 90% of anglers.

Whether it's freshwater or saltwater fishing, both involve a large amount of gear. When returning home after a trip, some of the equipment inevitably gets foreign debris on the car or leaks water... Anglers typically like to keep their vehicles clean, and incidents like this can quickly ruin your mood.

There is a great item to prevent vehicle trunk contamination and block any potential water leakage. It is the EVA Trunk Tray produced by Haedong. It comes in two sizes, and I ordered the Medium (M) size.

As shown in the image below, the trunk tray is literally a tray placed in your trunk. The Haedong EVA Trunk Tray features a generous length of 80 cm and a width of 50 cm.

Additionally, both sides feature molded carrying handles as shown in the picture below, making it easy to carry over short distances with both hands.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjhfMTQ0/MDAxNzkwNTgwOTA1NDg3.FfLtO-ETOv57koL787hbGKRcHaIaqRn0qzw4tH1zuwMg.9lZSmXK1cAiNmpluA_W9fd0p-FdIOOEf2eJXAF1UvCcg.JPEG/SE-91989e66-b306-11f1-8871-31684a54a46c.jpg?type=w800)

The width is 80 cm, exactly matching the ruler scale, the depth measures 50 cm, and the wall height is around 8 cm.

With a wall height of about 8 cm, which is quite ample, placing gear inside this tray keeps wet or dirty items contained, preventing anything from spilling onto the car trunk and keeping the vehicle pristine.

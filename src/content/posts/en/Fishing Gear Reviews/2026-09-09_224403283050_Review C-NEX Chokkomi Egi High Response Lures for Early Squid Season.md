### 3. Glow & Keimura UV Charging Comparison

Testing both under UV charging lights clearly reveals their tactical differences:
*(In the images below, the top lure is Fluorescent Green and the bottom is Gochujang Laser)*

* **Fluorescent Green:** Luminous glow is applied specifically to the tail hook and fins, while the entire body glows intensely under Keimura UV light.
* **Gochujang Laser:** Along with luminous tail hooks and fins, the body displays a powerful internal **bone-pattern skeleton glow**. However, its Keimura response is subtle.

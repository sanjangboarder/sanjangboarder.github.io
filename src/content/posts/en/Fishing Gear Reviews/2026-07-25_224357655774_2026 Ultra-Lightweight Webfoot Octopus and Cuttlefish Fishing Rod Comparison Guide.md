**Personal Recommendations & Summary**

If budget is the primary driver, the AliExpress Eustass is an unmatched bargain for beginners entering the sport.

Excluding budget-import options, my personal top pick for 2026 is the **Blue & Fish Idaco OC 150**. The combination of Toray T1100G carbon, Fuji guides, and a fast 9:1 action provides the ideal balance of sensitivity, durability, and performance for detecting delicate cuttlefish weight changes.

Pairing a 37g–40g rod with an ultra-lightweight baitcasting reel (120g–130g) creates a sub-170g total setup, dramatically reducing wrist fatigue during full-day charter sessions.

Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

There is a baitcasting reel that I acquired this year and have been using very well: the Daiwa Rize. Being a new release this year, it has enjoyed considerable popularity, so many anglers are likely familiar with it. While its stock handle measures 120mm, it leans strictly toward basic functionality rather than aesthetic style. Although there was nothing mechanically wrong with using it, I was looking for an attractive lightweight double handle to upgrade both its reeling smoothness and overall look.

What caught my eye was the BDH120-A24 Air lightweight double handle from GOMEXUS, designed primarily targeting reels like the Daiwa Admira or Shimano Stile. Of course, beyond Daiwa and Shimano, it is widely compatible with most reels from brands like Doyo, Abu Garcia, and others.

Showing the packaging first, Gomexus double handles clearly convey to customers: "We paid meticulous attention to quality!" Great effort has gone into the packaging presentation, giving you that feeling when unboxing that you simply can't bring yourself to throw the box away.

The internal components follow the standard configuration of Gomexus double handles. One pouch contains three nuts and various washers, another pouch holds the dedicated wrench used when attaching to the baitcasting reel, alongside the main double handle unit itself.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MTlfMTMw/MDAxNzg5NzczMTQ3NTMy.Qe6BEeFfwoDIlVVvxVv4t_P1YiuLvnP3cgsUv-J_Xqgg.2AevHPBhRZrb95lvC945aGv6ckuZ2siRb5poBCVbEWcg.JPEG/SE-b4d3f027-b306-11f1-8871-45bd4edc060d.jpg?type=w800)

The BDH120-A24 Air comes in two colors: one in silver, and the one I am showcasing here in black. While the overall tone of the handle is matte black, red accents are featured. There is a red accent color in the center of the handle plate and the retainer nut is also red, so it looks gorgeous even before mounting.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MTlfMTMx/MDAxNzg5NzczMzUwMDQ2.CoGoVp_4JsUdhhYjGO8uljHyl3PrbGyIFo13NH9YeLgg.3MuTI3ZidfAR-zbzo1F4plxG7hMxO5WtV8p3iV7Omn8g.JPEG/SE-b4d9957e-b306-11f1-8871-f313a57bdbed.jpg?type=w800)

If I were to name the biggest difference between Gomexus and cheap budget double handles, it is the superior knob quality. True to form, this BDH120-Air is crafted exceptionally well. Red accent rings are incorporated at both the top and bottom of the knobs. Looking at aesthetics alone, it ranks among the most attractive tuning double handles available.

Looking at the product specifications, each knob typically houses two bearings, and here Salt Guard bearings are employed, preventing rust or stiffness caused by saltwater intrusion over time. Even though it is an unseen internal component, taking care of such small details is truly impressive.

While specified as a 120mm handle, my own measurements show the center-to-center distance between knobs is 12cm, meaning that considering the knob dimensions, the perceived sweep feels akin to a 130mm double handle in practice. It weighs 29.2g; considering that most components use aviation aluminum, it is exceptionally lightweight.

The original stock weight of my Daiwa Rize baitcasting reel measured 186.5g. After tuning it with the Gomexus lightweight double handle, the total weight measured 189.1g—an increase of just 2.6g compared to Daiwa's stock double handle. Considering the substantial upgrade in performance, winding feel, and visual styling, this tiny difference is practically negligible.

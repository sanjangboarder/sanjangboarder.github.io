### 2. Design Features & Saltwater Durability

* **Dual A/B Push Buttons:** Allows independent counting (e.g., Side A for Webfoot Octopus, Side B for Cuttlefish).
* **Corrosion Resistance:** The push buttons are constructed from high-strength ABS plastic rather than rust-prone metal springs.
* **Drain Holes:** Built-in side drainage ports allow trapped saltwater to flush out easily, preventing salt buildup inside the internal mechanism.
* **Independent Reset Knobs:** Smooth 4-digit dials on both sides make resetting your daily tallies effortless.

Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

A wide variety of webfoot octopus and cuttlefish rods have hit the market this year. The prevailing trend has been extreme lightweight designs combined with softer 8:2 moderate-fast actions. Personally, I find fishing by direct tactile feel more intuitive than solely relying on visual tip watching; feeling the weight and water resistance transmitted straight into the palms is where a stiffer 9:1 fast action rod truly excels.

While an 8:2 rod offers advantages for detecting subtle visual changes at the tip, a 9:1 fast taper is distinctly superior when it comes to tactile sensation felt in the hand. Recently, rods with these specific attributes were either hard to find or couldn't genuinely be considered lightweight, but the newly released WindWeiser Iidako B160 TS rod fits the bill perfectly.

The tip section adopts a titanium tip renowned for excellent durability and high sensitivity. From the tip down to the 5th guide, high-visibility fluorescent thread wraps are applied, ensuring tip deflections are clearly readable by eye. For guide components, Fuji Alconite guides are installed, delivering solid corrosion resistance against saltwater environments. The guide inner diameter is well-suited for standard 0.8 to 1.0 PE braided lines.

The tip section weighs 17.2 g and the butt section weighs 38.2 g, resulting in a total measured weight of 55.4 g. This is roughly 2.4 g over catalog spec, but such tolerance is completely standard due to guide epoxies and reel seat bonding agents during assembly. While slightly heavier than featherweight rods hovering around 40 g, 55 g remains extremely lightweight in practical use, especially given the rigid 9:1 fast backbone.

Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Once the West Coast boat fishing season kicks off, I head out for webfoot octopus and cuttlefish fishing almost every single week. As regular visitors to my blog already know from my weekly trip reports, actively rotating through a diverse assortment of egis is a standard routine for me on both webfoot octopus and cuttlefish trips.

The reason I do this is that nobody can predict with absolute certainty which egi will work best on any given day, depending on changing sea conditions, weather, sunlight intensity, depth of the spot, water clarity, and tidal currents. Therefore, I establish priorities and systematically swap lures out when the bite slows down. Having already completed 4 outings this season, there was one particular egi that delivered an exceptionally striking response from webfoot octopus, which I would like to introduce to you today.

It is the UV Squid Rush egi from Haedong. Many anglers are probably unfamiliar with it, largely because Haedong does not heavily market their squid lures, leaving its reputation to spread quietly by word of mouth among in-the-know anglers. But because its bite rate has been so phenomenal, I decided to review it in detail.

There are about 10 colors in total in the lineup, and I will be showing you 5 of them. The colors of the UV Squid Rush egi are designated by numbers, and here are the ones I selected.

The standout feature of the UV Squid Rush egi is its Keimura (UV) fluorescence. You can visibly observe it glowing when illuminated with a UV light even under daylight conditions, and in dark conditions, the reaction becomes dramatically more pronounced. Among the colors shown, you can clearly see that the red head on the Gochujang and Watermelon patterns, as well as the Green body, produce an extremely potent Keimura reaction. This seems to be especially irresistible to webfoot octopuses, and I have high hopes that it will trigger great responses from cuttlefish as well starting in October.

Having fished all 5 colors across 4 separate outings this year alone, the most impressive performer was undeniably the Watermelon color. True to my expectations, it served as an all-weather workhorse, and I easily racked up 20+ consecutive hits with it multiple times. Whether on windy days, murky water, clear skies, or deep zones, its consistent bite response was remarkable. Following Watermelon, Gochujang and Green ranked next in effectiveness.

Nowadays, egis priced over 5,000 KRW are widely used, and I certainly deploy plenty of premium-priced lures myself. However, the Haedong UV Squid Rush egi holds its own against lures costing more than double—in fact, it often elicited superior bite responses, rightfully earning its spot on my favorite lures list for this season. For those seeking outstanding cost-performance egis, this is definitely one worth picking up, which is why I gladly recommend it.

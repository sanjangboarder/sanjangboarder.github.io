Thus, bare rod weight is **75.9g**, and with the rod saver cap included, it weighs **90.0g**. This shows significant weight savings compared to previous JS Company squid rod models!

More important than pure weight is rod balance. Without a reel attached, the unmounted balance point sits **directly in front of the reel seat**, offering superior balance setup compared to rival lightweight rods.

### 2. Glass Solid Tip Action & Reel Combination

The manufacturer lists the optimal sinker rating as #15 to #20.

​

<div class="single-image">
<img src="https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MjlfMTY1/MDAxNzg8MDA1Mjk5Njcw.5WB6Dpe5zZpsDjD7BXREm5qjMbVdCfs2NJTkJ6ZFpxsg.88X4Gq4d7VNafEpZIQ1XWsKmoO1n2bn3lhtdYWXtmIIg.PNG/image.png?type=w800" />
</div>

​

However, because the glass solid tip offers extreme flexibility, using **#10 to #14 sinkers** delivers maximum sensitivity satisfaction. Describing the taper action as 8:2, it transmits subtle micro-bites even more delicately than titanium-tip 8:2 rods.

### 3. Field Testing Results on Opening Day (Sept 1st)

I brought this rod directly onboard Valkyrie 7 out of Ocheon Port on September 1st. Its compact packability made navigating crowded dock areas effortless without bumping into anything.

During the entire morning session, the glass solid tip's high sensitivity shone brightly—it transmitted even tiny "runny nose-sized" baby squid weight shifts with crystal clarity, letting me hook even micro squid!

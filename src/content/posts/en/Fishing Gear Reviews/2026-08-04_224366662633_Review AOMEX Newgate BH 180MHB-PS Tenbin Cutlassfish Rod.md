### 1. Specifications & Weight Measurements

* **Length:** 1.80m (2-piece center-cut spigot joint).
* **Target Sinker Weight:** 30 to 80 (approx. 110g – 300g Tenbin sinkers).
* **Guides:** Genuine Fuji K-series Alconite ring guide setup.
* **Measured Weight:** Tip Section: 22.1g + Butt Section: 45.2g = **67.4g Total Weight**!
* **Diameters:** Tip diameter: 1.27mm / Butt diameter: 10.4mm.

### 2. Action Taper & Balance Evaluation

* **7:3 Action Taper:** The sensitive top 30% section detects delicate nibbles from suspended cutlassfish, while the lower 70% backbone provides solid power to lift 4-finger+ (4지) trophy cutlassfish.
* **Ergonomic Balance:** When mounted with a baitcasting reel, the balance center sits directly at the grip hand position, minimizing wrist fatigue during all-night charter trips.

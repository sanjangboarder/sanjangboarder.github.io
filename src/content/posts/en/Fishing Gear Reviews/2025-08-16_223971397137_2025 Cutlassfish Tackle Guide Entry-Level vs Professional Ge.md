**Entry-Level Setup (Budget: 100k–150k KRW):**
* **Rod:** 7.6ft–8.0ft Light Inshore/Rockfish rod (~50k KRW)
* **Reel:** 2000-size spinning reel spooled with PE 0.6 braid (~50k KRW)
* **Verdict:** Perfect for beginners who fish shore breakwaters 3–5 times a season.

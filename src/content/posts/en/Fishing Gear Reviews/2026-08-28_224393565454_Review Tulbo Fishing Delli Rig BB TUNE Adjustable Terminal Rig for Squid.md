### 1. Why Pick BB TUNE Over Previous Delli Rigs?

While previous 1st and 2nd Gen Delli Rigs allowed both sinker dropper height and branch line length to slide, on actual boat charters, anglers modify **branch line length** far more frequently depending on tide velocity and squid activity.

The **BB TUNE (BB 튠)** version perfectly refines this: sinker dropper height is kept fixed while the branch line length can be freely adjusted **from 0cm up to 60cm**. This streamlined setup greatly reduces line tangle stress on deck.

My main picks for this season are the **20cm branch line** and **0cm branch line** options. 
* **20cm Branch Line:** Allows you to cut and trim line length down to customize dropper distance freely on the water.
* **0cm Branch Line (Zero Dropper):** Extremely popular in octopus angling. Even with 0cm dropper length, lifting your sinker slightly off the bottom gives complete manual control over lure presentation.

### 3. Knot Adjustment Mechanism & 5-号 Ester Line Tips

Adjusting branch line length is simple:
* **Longer Knot loops** lock the dropper distance firmly.
* **Shorter Knot loops** allow smooth sliding adjustments.

**Ester Line Straightening Tip:**
The rig uses heavy-duty **Size 5 (5-号) Ester line**. When unpacked, the line might look slightly coiled. Hold both ends of the leader and pull firmly for 2 seconds—the Ester line will magically straighten laser-straight, maximizing bite sensitivity underwater!

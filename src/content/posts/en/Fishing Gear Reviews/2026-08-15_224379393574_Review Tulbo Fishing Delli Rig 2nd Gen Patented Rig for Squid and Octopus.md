### 2. Key Technology: Patented Adjustable Friction Knots

The standout innovation of the 2nd Gen Delli Rig lies in its **dual-color sliding friction knots**:

​

1. **Black Ester Line:** Built using tangle-resistant black ester line that stays low-profile underwater.
2. **Transparent Friction Knot:** Loosening or sliding this knot adjusts the **dropper branch line length** from a direct lure attachment all the way to a long free-floating leader.
3. **Pink Friction Knot:** Controls the **sinker height (dropper distance)** off the sea floor.

### 3. Tacticals & Versatility

* **Active Bite Conditions:** Shorten the branch line for fast sensitivity and crisp hooksets.
* **Fussy / Suspended Squid:** Lengthen the branch line for a slow, natural fluttering descent (falling action).
* **Free-Rig Setup:** Leaving the friction knot un-cinched allows the branch swivel to slide freely along the main line like a free-rig setup.

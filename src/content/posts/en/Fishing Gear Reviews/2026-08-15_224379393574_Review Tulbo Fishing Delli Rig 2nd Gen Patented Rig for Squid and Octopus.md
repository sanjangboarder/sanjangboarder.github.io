### 1. Specification Options & Packaging Breakdown

When ordering the Delli Rig 2nd Gen, you can customize the main leader length, branch line length, and swivel snap types. 
* **My Selection:** 70cm Main Line / 25cm Branch Line / Double Pin Swaps.
* **Main Line Selection Guide:** A 50cm to 70cm main line is ideal for West Coast shallow-to-medium water depths. For deep-sea charter grounds off Jeju or the South Coast, a 100cm main line provides maximum adjustment range.

### 2. Patented Adjustable Knot Mechanism

The core invention of the Delli Rig lies in its **variable friction knots**:

​

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MTVfMjk5/MDAxNzg6NzYzNTA3NzY2.ANcjmAt9N5-xf0k80gfQTV5rDifJEdONaWyFOvgIdDkg.5EJfcrNh2sk1J2CbYrD6e40cE8oIsGT0kItpEMeF9q8g.JPEG/SE-25e0e0f3-9856-11f1-86cd-e1fe0d343183.jpg?type=w800)
![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MTVfMTU0/MDAxNzg6NzYzNTA3MTYw.2L7gZ0rnvJVnidtNKkrtev1sePXFnWJz2HAVvK7lKMAg.DM0mzLYduYq-WaIEMDlnA6wnaWgSxWWC0E3Hpf0PGEog.JPEG/SE-25e17d34-9856-11f1-86cd-4fc499a5a83f.jpg?type=w800)

​

* **Clear Knot:** Tightening or loosening the clear knot allows the branch swivel to slide freely or lock firmly, adjusting the effective branch line length.

​

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MTVfMjYy/MDAxNzg6NzYzMTIzNDQ4.zI9f-Tnk7B2NJPgS-Jl5RlyMqlDsGfnK45zYhY6lvxAg.L9YgUnHO1Wt89qDcTPFVcJEuyzkHSr8kkeJZc0HtFqwg.JPEG/900_20260814_145000.jpg?type=w800)
![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MTVfMjUg/MDAxNzg6NzYzMTI0OTc4.hqaxAevQjfi5fP-EAlDn-Zy3VykqjFowEF11TpXxF4gg.ZH-_HpcBVGoGPXZsWAFu6QDLnDWkbUsz_q3cwWQj_zQg.JPEG/900_20260814_145010.jpg?type=w800)

​

* **Pink Knot:** Sliding the pink knot adjusts the distance (dropper height) between the sinker bottom and the branch line.

​

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MTVfMTY1/MDAxNzg6NzYzMTIyNDkz.gzZEJ4IUJ0xMbs7neort8gcz1zSJrMG-DBOHnWhIeVEg.iW0SaKURsZdh8riH74gmE-hypTlWSBmAB1J3GB1z3YIg.JPEG/900_20260814_145055.jpg?type=w800)
![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA8MTVfMTA4/MDAxNzg6NzYzMTI0Nzgw.r8saz0SOSa7HJPbv59mXw0pzPVrK_tSVDwnlaq6MUA8g.Ejtb8jE_udBptu_-Cw8JkN2OLFVmSUbGyaTmdu2m7dUg.JPEG/900_20260814_145106.jpg?type=w800)

​

By sliding these two knots, you can seamlessly convert your setup anywhere from a **zero-dropper direct rig** (for active octopus) to a **long-branch floating rig** (for shy cuttlefish requiring natural drift).

​

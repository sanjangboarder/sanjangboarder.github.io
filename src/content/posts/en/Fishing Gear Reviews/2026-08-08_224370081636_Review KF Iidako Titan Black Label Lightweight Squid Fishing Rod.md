### 1. Actual Weight & Balance Measurement

* **Manufacturer Specification:** 68g.
* **Measured Weight:** **66.4g** total (Tip Section: 13.8g + Butt Section: 52.6g)—a full 1.6g lighter than advertised!
* **Balance Point:** The balance center sits directly in front of the reel seat, ensuring zero tip-heaviness when holding the rod for full 8-hour charter sessions.

* **Blank Action:** 9:1 Fast Action taper featuring a stiff carbon backbone with a high-sensitivity titanium alloy tip section.
* **Sensitivity Performance:** The titanium tip transmits subtle weight changes when a small octopus latches onto the squid jig (Egi), allowing quick, decisive hooksets.

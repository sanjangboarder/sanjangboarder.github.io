### 1. Water-Pressure Sensor Mechanism & Safety Specs

Unlike standard automatic inflators that trigger upon contact with rain or sea spray, the HS-X06 features a specialized **Hydrostatic Water-Pressure Inflator**:

​

* **Submersion Trigger:** Automatically inflates within 5 to 10 seconds only when submerged in water deeper than 10cm, preventing accidental inflation from rain or boat splashes.
* **Emergency Features:** Includes an emergency whistle, oral inflation mouthpiece, and reflective tape bands for night search-and-rescue visibility.
* **Self-Righting Inflation:** When inflated, the internal bladder expands upwards around the chest and neck, keeping the angler's head safely above water even if unconscious.

### 1. Ultra-Light Weight Measurements & Specs

Heavy-duty octopus rods rarely dip under 100g, making AOMEX's build quality stand out:

​

* **Length:** 1.72m (2-piece center-cut spigot joint).
* **Measured Weight:** Tip Section: 25.9g + Butt Section: 44.7g = **70.5g Total Weight**!
* **Diameters:** Tip diameter: 1.44mm / Butt diameter: 11.23mm.
* **Max Sinker Load:** Rated for 30 to 50-size sinkers (up to 200g).

### 2. 8:2 Heavy Action Taper & Balance

* **8:2 Heavy Action:** Stiff, powerful carbon backbone engineered to peel heavy 1kg+ octopus off rocky bottoms without flexing out.
* **Zero Tip-Heaviness:** Even before mounting a baitcasting reel, the balance center sits directly in front of the reel seat, keeping arm fatigue to an absolute minimum during 8-hour trips.

### 3. Comparison with Previous Conical Straighteners

Comparing this against my previous plastic-body conical corrector: while the old model could theoretically adjust any hook size depending on applied pressure, hook points frequently dug into the soft groove, getting stuck.

Because this new corrector is solid machined stainless steel, hook points cannot gauge into the metal, restoring bent hooks into perfect conical alignments smoothly.

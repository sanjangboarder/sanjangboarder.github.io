### 1. Innovation: Built-in Dual Head Hook

Predatory cutlassfish often attack the head or mid-section of prey rather than trailing the tail.

​

* **Dual Hook Design:** Unlike traditional single-hook reverse jigheads, 153 Craft includes an **integrated extra hook along the top head section**.
* **Eliminates Extra Rigging:** Removes the hassle of tying cumbersome stinger hooks or treble assist traces.
* **Hybrid Bait Option:** The top hook can also be tipped with a strip of cut bait (saury or cutlassfish skin) for hybrid lure-plus-fresh-bait action.

Available in **5g, 11g, and 15g** specs, these jigheads are tailored for long-distance casting along seawalls such as Saemangeum.

* **Hook Length:** ~5.5cm across all weights, ideal for 3-inch soft plastics.
* **Measured Weights:** 5.2g, 11.4g, and 15.3g (slightly heavier than spec for improved casting distance).

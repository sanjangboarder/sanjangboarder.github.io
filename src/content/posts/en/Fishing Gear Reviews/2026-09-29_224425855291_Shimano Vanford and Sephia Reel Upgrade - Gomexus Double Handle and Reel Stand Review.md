Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Hearing that Gomexus released the Air-Sense 98, a lightweight 98mm double handle designed specifically for eging spinning reels, I decided to pick one up. It paired perfectly with my Shimano Vanford and Sephia reels, so I would like to show you a detailed look at how it performs and looks.

To begin with, this product was released under the name Air-Sense 98 as a specialized, lightweight double handle for squid and cuttlefish eging. When considering spinning reels renowned for eging, Daiwa's Emeraldas immediately comes to mind, and naturally, Gomexus offers an Emeraldas colorway as well. However, since I operate Shimano's dedicated eging reel Sephia and also wanted to use it on the similarly styled Vanford, I selected the color below.

Gomexus has consistently introduced high-quality double handles for spinning reels featuring sophisticated designs. This product also delivers a premium experience right from its packaging. Spinning reel handles are not cross-compatible between Shimano and Daiwa, so be sure to choose the correct model for your reel brand.

The basic setup contains no weights inside and has a rubber ring in the center, representing the lightest configuration. You can utilize this setting when your primary goal is simply having a protective stand.

To calibrate the reel's lateral balance, you can insert weights into the cavity. Up to 6 weights can be loaded; each weighs 0.8g, totaling an additional 4.8g. Spinning reels allow either left- or right-hand retrieve, but whichever side is set, the reel body tilts toward the handle's weight. Offsetting this off-center weight distribution is one of the key functions of the reel stand.

* **Bare Reel Body Weight (without handle):** **105.6g** (Total factory stock weight: 125.6g).

Combining the KF Iidako Rod and Reel creates a **full sub-200g boat tackle setup** for under 250,000 KRW. Replacing heavy legacy 250g+ setups with this sub-200g gear dramatically reduces arm fatigue over an 8-hour trip while transmitting subtle squid weight changes with incredible clarity.

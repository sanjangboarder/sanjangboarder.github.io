Measuring the weight of the KF 130mm ultra-lightweight double handle, it differs by **only 0.2g** compared to the factory-installed handle.

Even though the knobs are larger and smoother, and the handle length is 10mm longer, having virtually identical weight means they paid maximum attention to weight reduction when crafting it... After tuning with the double handle, total weight came out to **125.8g**, which is almost identical to pre-tuning weight, effectively upgrading cranking feel alone.

Combining the KF Iidako rod and baitcasting reel allows a full setup in the low 200,000 KRW range. At this price point and weight class, I think it will be a great choice for beginners starting out this year.

​

Looking up baitcasting reel weights, plenty of products exceed 200g on their own. In the past, most anglers used setups exceeding 250g, and often over 300g. Once you try a 200g total setup, you will immediately realize why lightweight gear is the trend.

​

Due to the nature of full-day squid boat fishing, it makes a massive difference in fatigue, and the sensitivity that lightness brings offers a distinct advantage in fishing. Thus, not only beginners, but also anglers who used heavy setups over 250g will likely experience high satisfaction after trying this configuration.

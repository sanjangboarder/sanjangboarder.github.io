### 2. 130mm Ultra-Lightweight Carbon Double Handle Tuning

To increase cranking leverage without adding unwanted weight, I upgraded the stock handle to the **KF 130mm Carbon Double Handle**:

​

* **Weight Comparison:** Stock handle (20.0g) vs. 130mm Carbon Handle (20.2g)—a negligible 0.2g difference despite offering wider knobs and a 10mm longer arm length!
* **Result:** Total reel weight with the upgraded 130mm handle remains **125.8g**, providing significantly smoother, effortless cranking power.

### Conclusion

Conventional octopus setups frequently exceed 250–300g. Reducing overall gear weight under 200g dramatically improves bottom sensitivity while eliminating fatigue during full-day charter sessions. In the 200,000 KRW price tier, this KF combination offers exceptional value for beginner and intermediate anglers alike.

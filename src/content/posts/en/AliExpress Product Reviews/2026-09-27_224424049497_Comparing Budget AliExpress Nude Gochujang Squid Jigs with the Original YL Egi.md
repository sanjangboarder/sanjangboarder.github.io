Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Among the squid jigs I frequently use for webfoot octopus and cuttlefish fishing, there is the YL (Yeollin) Nude Gochujang (red head nude body). While browsing AliExpress, I noticed they were selling lures that look remarkably similar at a very cheap price... The price was about 5,750 KRW for a pack of 5, which comes out to around 1,150 KRW each—an extraordinarily low price that piqued my curiosity. For reference, the genuine Yeollin Nude Gochujang usually costs around 4,000 KRW each, making the AliExpress version more than three times cheaper.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfMzUg/MDAxNzkwNDg2MDAyMzE0.WW5ZRGqxev-ztJAtjgV6iw4rUeAi51CmOxW2uU5IVM4g.D1gF6k6Z1ubJTJEiRDqtqk5P5-xai_LUlMuTiTthxYwg.PNG/image.png?type=w800)

So out of sheer curiosity, I decided to be the guinea pig and placed an order for the Nude Gochujang along with two other colors to test for webfoot octopus and cuttlefish fishing.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfMjE5/MDAxNzkwNDg2MzgwNDEy.0RiMijfJoLGxOciGtIvC8XFcZ4SGlFoNpDLUqWS94TMg.8Q2BfM2bx4V4ddXm5PMVgBqqVIekYF92i3UyTpQHp7Qg.PNG/image.png?type=w800)

It seems that identical lures to the budget Nude Gochujang egi I bought on AliExpress are also sold on domestic Korean shopping malls. They look almost identical to models like the B-Black Mini Egi, Mongwol Mini Dropper, or C-NEX Korea Kkoma Egi 75, so the possibility that they come from the very same factory cannot be ruled out. Domestically, they usually sell for around 9,500 KRW for a pack of 5 or about 2,500 KRW each, which is roughly twice as expensive as AliExpress.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfMjU1/MDAxNzkwNDg2NDE1MjEy.42pbhC8suIeaVnpFkDt1UbFo0BLJ46_H5lyoc1IB48Qg.rjReJt_xcOhvs_-kFIhUug156wEM22DnZkfAv0h3Qmwg.PNG/image.png?type=w800)

Standard / Glow Luminescence / Keimura UV

Now, here is a comparison with the authentic original YL Nude Gochujang. All my other Nude Gochujang lures were lost in action during previous battles, leaving only one used original lure for comparison. Placing them side by side, definite differences immediately become visible.

First, the swivel size is slightly different. The eye design is completely different. Also, looking closely, the authentic Nude Gochujang body features ribbed textured stripe lines that create a tactile finish, whereas the AliExpress direct-import lure body is completely smooth. There is also a difference in hooks: the hooks on the YL Nude Gochujang feel somewhat sharper, and the spacing between the two hook tiers is slightly shorter.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfMTI5/MDAxNzkwNDg5NzU5NjQ3.LXISVqyFtc0smMdqYnkfN-KtHKrRfB4o2jmRrlCRerEg.4uon99T-9gyOR1VbFRJgn_hUFR3LATyiCYID7khX6gAg.JPEG/SE-8f7193ff-a9eb-4210-895c-45626d7a7e7b.jpg?type=w800)
![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfNjMg/MDAxNzkwNDg5NzQ2ODg0.Uyqbsa9w3CpJ93JOAk5NqHE5h6JyBkWaSbf7Qk7G4ZAg.Pf6e_A30SkQYPCc_6XnDSyrsQ9U693nU2qAWfVBjInsg.JPEG/SE-684b922b-ba32-11f1-8f12-dfc04d85ff2e.jpg?type=w800)

Luminescence performance—which often decides results during early dawn hours of webfoot octopus fishing—is somewhat hard to discern in bright photo settings, but noticeable differences exist. On the YL egi, the fin tinsel features a strong glow effect, and the eyes also glow. In contrast, the AliExpress Nude Gochujang has no glow in its eyes and no glow on its fin tinsel. Another major difference lies in the intensity of the Keimura UV effect, which is critical in deep water and cuttlefish angling: the YL Nude Gochujang showed an overwhelmingly brighter reaction.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfMSAg/MDAxNzkwNDg5NzI3ODI5.kfeItDaeRiiO4uANLjawsseQkMlBx8ka5dfEkhGSfO0g.IdvwwZGcUEEmJfZmG3a6-Qdya5COGGquQkY6g_XetZ4g.JPEG/SE-684eed8e-ba32-11f1-8f12-19951fe02d14.jpg?type=w800)
![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjdfMTQy/MDAxNzkwNDg5NzI3MzAy.fRpFY5QN5E_PABmU3NZ6s1YTTo4phKxI5WAvIlK7oCQg.lmU4YbqYgHn_N2ku0QAfzusDx8pV5-sR_fNVUJmo0h8g.JPEG/SE-684e2a3d-ba32-11f1-8f12-13c20ea68cf8.jpg?type=w800)

To summarize, while at first glance it might seem there is little difference compared to the authentic Nude Gochujang... a closer look reveals definite discrepancies in the fine details. With a price difference of more than three times, the original offers glowing eyes, glowing fin tinsel, superior body luminescence and Keimura fluorescence, as well as finer hooks; however, whether those differences justify a three-fold price gap remains up for debate.

Whether these small differences will significantly impact catch numbers in real field conditions is something I honestly cannot say for sure yet. Since I have several octopus and cuttlefish trips left this season, I will test them thoroughly and report the results through upcoming fishing logs. If you are interested, please stay tuned for those forthcoming reports.

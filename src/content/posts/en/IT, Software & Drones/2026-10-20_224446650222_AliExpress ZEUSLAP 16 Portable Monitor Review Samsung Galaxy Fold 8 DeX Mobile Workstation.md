Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Using the Samsung Galaxy Fold 8 with its main screen unfolded provides an expansive viewing canvas that makes watching videos, reading ebooks, and web browsing remarkably comfortable. Recently, however, I set up a mobile environment to practice AI-assisted programming anytime, anywhere, and navigating this workflow directly on the Fold 8 display felt somewhat constrained.

I host an Ubuntu virtual machine on Amazon Lightsail and run Antigravity's CLI interface, Agy. Because it is a CLI (Command Line Interface) environment, work takes place primarily inside terminal console windows. In this setup, even the Fold 8's unfolded screen felt cramped, sparking my desire to output Samsung DeX onto a spacious 16-inch portable monitor.

Searching across portable monitor options quickly revealed that the leading brand in this budget category is ZEUSLAP. Looking into their 16-inch lineup, there were numerous configurations balancing resolution, brightness, touch input, and connectivity ports. To keep things clean and functional, I ruled out high 2.5K/4K resolutions because UI fonts would appear too diminutive, and opted for a non-touch model engineered for peak panel brightness.

I purchased the monitor directly via AliExpress for just under 120,000 KRW (roughly \$90 USD)—an exceptionally attractive price point. Shipping was surprisingly swift, clearing customs and arriving at my doorstep in under a week. Because it is sensitive consumer electronics, the packaging was thoughtfully reinforced against transit shock.

You receive one mini-HDMI to standard HDMI cable and two USB Type-C cables. The HDMI cable connects readily to laptops, mini PCs, and gaming consoles, while the USB-C cables serve both charging and unified display-plus-power connections for smartphones and modern notebooks.

The manufacturer supplies an AC charging brick with a bundled plug converter. The charger outputs up to 18W over Power Delivery (PD)—not ultra-fast charging, but perfectly calibrated to power the monitor stably. When shopping on AliExpress, electronics come in diverse regional plug standards. Some buyers mistakenly select EU plugs thinking they match Korea's 220V standard; however, EU pins are slightly thinner (4.0mm vs 4.8mm) and wobble inside Korean sockets, causing electrical sparking or fire hazards. I strongly recommend choosing the US 110V plug and using a snug 110V-to-220V grounded adapter. Notice in the middle photo that the seller thoughtfully included this exact configuration.

In practical use, what makes this ZEUSLAP monitor so satisfying is that connecting it via a single USB-C cable directly to the Samsung Galaxy Fold 8 powers up the monitor without needing an external outlet. Pair a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard to the Fold 8 as pictured, and you immediately enjoy a full desktop-class workstation anywhere.

However, one operational nuance should be noted: while the Fold 8's battery can drive the monitor autonomously, brightness is restricted to about one-third of its capability. Increasing brightness beyond that threshold triggers a reboot loop because the phone's power output is limited. To achieve full 100% panel luminance, simply connect the included AC adapter or a power bank to the monitor's secondary USB-C port. With dedicated power supplied, the display shines brilliantly at maximum brightness.

Initially, because I planned to navigate exclusively with a mouse and keyboard, I thought touchscreen functionality would be superfluous. In real-world terminal and IDE usage, however, I immediately recognized the benefit of a touchscreen: two-finger pinch-to-zoom makes adjusting terminal font sizes instantaneous, whereas managing that purely with a mouse is cumbersome. If I were purchasing again today, I would strongly consider the touch-enabled variant.

Having utilized this setup for roughly a month, I cannot imagine reverting to a life without this portable monitor. Its generous screen real estate transforms productivity, and its lightweight profile slips into any backpack with less bulk and weight than a typical laptop or heavy tablet. Accompanied by a slim portable keyboard and mouse, it effectively replaces the need to carry a dedicated laptop.

Beyond Samsung DeX, it hooks effortlessly into gaming consoles like the Nintendo Switch or Sony PlayStation, and doubles as an ideal secondary display alongside a laptop when working from cafes or client sites.

Considering its remarkably accessible price, anyone seeking an affordable, flexible external screen should definitely keep this model on their radar.

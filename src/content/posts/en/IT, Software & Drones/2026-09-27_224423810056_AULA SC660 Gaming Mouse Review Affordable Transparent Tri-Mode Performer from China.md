Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

This time, I'm sharing a review after using one of my current mice for several months that I found quite impressive: the AULA SC660 mouse (often called 'Dokgeomi' or 'Tarantula' in Korea). Dokgeomi is the popular Korean nickname for the brand AULA, which is a Chinese peripheral manufacturer.

Just four to five years ago, I firmly believed that Microsoft or Logitech were the only true answers for mice and keyboards, and I used products exclusively from those brands... However, after recently purchasing and actively using three AULA keyboards, and trying out mice from Chinese brands like AULA and Glorious, my perspective has shifted considerably.

Decent offerings from Chinese brands have now reached a remarkably high level in terms of build quality and usability. There is no longer any strict necessity to insist only on traditional input device giants like Logitech or Microsoft.

Domestic Korean resellers importing the AULA SC660 typically price it in the low-to-mid 40,000 KRW range. If you purchase it directly from China, you can get it for several thousand won cheaper, and it is the exact same product.

I opted for direct purchase from China, and the purchase link is provided at the bottom of the article for reference—it is a few thousand won cheaper than domestic sellers.

The AULA SC660 is an ultra-lightweight, high-performance mouse that supports versatile connection modes.

The mouse weighs 80 grams, which puts it firmly on the lightweight side. The overall length is approximately 11 cm, which is standard mouse sizing. Nowadays, lightweight engineering has essentially become standard practice... Rather than making it overly compact just to reduce weight, it retains standard mouse proportions to maintain a comfortable grip.

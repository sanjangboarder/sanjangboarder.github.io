This weekend, I originally booked an offshore boat fishing charter out on the West Coast for rockfish. However, due to strong gale warnings and rough high waves, the charter was unfortunately cancelled. Disappointed but unwilling to sit idle at home, I quickly packed my light tackle and made an emergency run to **Seonjae Fishing Park** in Incheon for a night lure fishing session.

### 1. Arrival & Facility Updates

Arriving around 5:00 PM, I checked in at the main management office for a 6-hour night pass. I noticed the cafe and rest facility area had been newly renovated into a comfortable 24-hour lounge bar setup where anglers can warm up, order hot drinks, and take breaks between fishing sessions.

​

After securing my entry, I set up my gear on **Pontoon A (A )**, which offers optimal casting angles toward the central floating net-cage structures ( ).

​

---

### 2. Rod & Tackle Setup

For targeting net-cages during night sessions, light lure tackle provides the highest sensitivity for detecting delicate bites along shadow lines:

​

* **Short-Range Setup:** 6.3ft Ultra-Light (UL) Spinning Rod + 2000-size Spinning Reel + #0.6 PE Braided Line + #1.5 Fluorocarbon Leader.
* **Long-Range Setup:** 7.6ft Light (L) Spinning Rod + 2500-size Spinning Reel + #0.8 PE Braided Line + #2.0 Fluorocarbon Leader.
* **Lures & Rig:** 1/16oz to 1/8oz Jigheads paired with 2-inch to 3-inch Pink & White Soft Grub Worms.

### 3. Night Session Tacticals & Catch Progress

​

#### A Sudden Rod Breakage Incident
Early into the session, disaster struck! While casting a 1/8oz jighead toward the net-cage ropes, my primary 7.6ft UL rod tip snapped unexpectedly near the 2nd guide joint. Luckily, I always carry a spare rod in my vehicle—a **Haedong Shadow** rod—which allowed me to resume fishing without breaking my momentum.

#### Net-Cage Shadow Line Strikes
As midnight approached, water flow around the cages slowed, and fish began feeding actively along the dark boundary lines created by the facility spotlights. 

​

* **Targeting 50cm Depth:** By pitching 1/16oz jigheads right up against the floating foam barrels of Pontoon C and E and hopping the worm roughly 50cm below the surface, aggressive **Rockfish (rockfish)** slammed the lure.
* **Red Sea Bream Activity:** Surprisingly, despite early spring water temperatures, active **Red Sea Breams (red sea bream)** were also biting near the mid-water column, showing that spring feeding season is officially under way.

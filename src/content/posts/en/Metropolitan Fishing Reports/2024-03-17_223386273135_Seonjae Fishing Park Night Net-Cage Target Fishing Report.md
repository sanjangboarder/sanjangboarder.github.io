### Tackle & Rig Setup

For night lure fishing at Seonjae, light tackle yields the highest sensitivity for detecting subtle bites near structure:

* **Short Range Rod:** Ultra-Light (UL) spin rod (6.0ft) paired with 2000-size spinning reel and #0.6 PE line.
* **Long Range Rod:** Light (L) spin rod (7.6ft) paired with 2500-size spinning reel and #0.8 PE line.
* **Rig:** 1/16oz to 1/8oz jigheads paired with 2-inch to 3-inch pink/white grub worms and live unsewn float rigs.

### Night Session Highlights & Catch Report

As darkness settled, active fish began congregating under the shadow lines of the net-cages.

​

1. **Targeting the Net-Cage Shadows:** By casting 1/8oz jigheads right alongside the outer ropes of the floating cages and allowing a slow 5-second drop, I landed consecutive fat Rockfish (rockfish).
2. **Midnight Feeding Window:** Between 10:00 PM and midnight, fish activity peaked. Slow swimming retrieves with pink grub worms triggered solid bites from both Rockfish and early Red Sea Breams.

​

Overall, the spontaneous night session at Seonjae turned out to be a resounding success with over a dozen quality fish landed before packing up at dawn.

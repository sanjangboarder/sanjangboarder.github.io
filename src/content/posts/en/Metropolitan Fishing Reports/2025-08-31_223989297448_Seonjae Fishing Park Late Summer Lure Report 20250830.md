On August 30th, I visited **Seonjae Fishing Park**, a managed saltwater fishing park on Seonjaedo Island, for a late summer lure session.

​

​

Late summer water temperatures bring high fish activity. Using 1/4oz metal jigs and 3-inch shad tail worms on light spinning tackle yielded energetic strikes around structure zones.

​

​

I landed several strong Red Sea Breams and Japanese Sea Bass. The water quality and fish condition at Seonjae Fishing Park were top notch.

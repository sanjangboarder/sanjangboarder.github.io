Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Over this holiday weekend, I have been going on three consecutive fishing trips accompanied by acquaintances, friends, and beginners. Personally, I considered this one of the major assignments of this season, and thankfully everything wrapped up smoothly without a hitch. The final mission was introducing two beginners to sea fishing—my wife and her friend.

In fact, when it comes to introducing complete novices to webfoot octopus (jjukkumi) fishing, I believe that hourly trips aboard large steel vessels—such as Kukje Yuseon's Happy-ho operating out of Incheon Yeonan Pier or Namhang Port—are by far the most suitable. The accessibility from the metropolitan area is excellent, and in my experience, most beginners or those looking for a casual trial experience find full-day boat schedules quite overwhelming. For newcomers eager to try octopus fishing, the morning half-day boat out of Incheon Namhang Port with Kukje Yuseon offers around 4 hours of fishing in a stable, comfortable environment aboard a large vessel, making it truly ideal.

On October 5, I arrived at Incheon Namhang Port before 4:00 AM to secure parking for the morning charter. But surprisingly, the parking lot was already open. When I checked why, it turned out that all 9.77-ton 22-passenger high-speed vessels from both Yeonan Pier and Namhang Port had canceled their trips due to gale-force winds, leaving the port area remarkably uncrowded. Because of this, I thought parking would remain totally spacious, but as shown in the picture below, once the main lot opened around 5:10 AM, it quickly filled right up.

During this lull, webfoot octopus and cuttlefish emerged quite generously. The water depth was in the 3 to 4-meter range, so the skipper of Happy-ho did a fantastic job targeting forgiving shallow spots where beginners could fish without strain.

Had it been an all-day charter, they surely would have complained of exhaustion, making hourly half-day trips fantastic for occasions like this. Both were initially worried about seasickness, but aside from taking an Aneron motion sickness pill beforehand, Happy-ho's sheer size keeps rolling to a minimum even in harsh winds, so seasickness concerns are virtually nonexistent.

We returned to Namhang Port cleanly right at 11:00 AM. Having the entire afternoon open to spend as you please is another significant perk of morning half-day boats.

Webfoot octopus fishing offers tremendous satisfaction for newcomers, which is why so many beginners search for it among Incheon boat fishing options. Kukje Yuseon's Happy-ho has two friendly deckhands onboard who provide warm, attentive guidance whenever you visit, ensuring a welcoming environment to start your sea fishing journey.

The two novices who came along with me are already pestering me to book our next trip haha. Towards late October, I plan to recruit one more beginner for a 4-person party and report on how we fare. Additionally, starting this week, I have three consecutive weekly octopus and cuttlefish trips lined up in the Chungnam region. Weather permitting, I will bring you fresh news from Mageompo and Ocheon Port.

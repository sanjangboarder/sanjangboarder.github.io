Hello, this is SanjangBorder.

​

While white croaker is usually my main summer focus, there was one target species I definitely wanted to challenge this season: **Sea Bass Oesujil (live shrimp drifting) boat fishing off Incheon**. On July 26th, I finally boarded **Yeongjin-ho** departing from Sammok Port on Yeongjongdo Island.

​

Located under 30 minutes from my home in Songdo, Sammok Port is an extremely convenient local fishing hub near Incheon International Airport.

**Tackle Setup:**
* **Rod:** AOMAX Navigator DS-180B
* **Reel:** Creman Little Giant Baitcasting Reel
* **Line:** Haedong Limit X9 PE 1.5 Braid
* **Rig:** Haedong Ball-Bearing Oesujil Rig + Size #22 Sea Bass Hooks
* **Bait:** Live Shrimp

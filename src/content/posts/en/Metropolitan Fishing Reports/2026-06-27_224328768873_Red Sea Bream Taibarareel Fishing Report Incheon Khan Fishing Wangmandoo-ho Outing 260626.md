### 1. Morning Action & Surprise Monster Rockfish

Bites were sparse early on, requiring consistent re-bottoming and steady 10-to-15 turn handle retrieves.

​

#### The 40cm+ Rockfish Strike
During a slow 15-turn retrieve off the seabed, a aggressive bite struck mid-water. The heavy drag pull felt like a solid snapper at first, but upon bringing it to the surface, it turned out to be a massive **40cm+ Giant Rockfish (개우럭)**!

### 2. Afternoon Grind & Landing Target Red Sea Bream

After a quick lunch on deck, afternoon fishing remained tough across the entire Incheon fleet due to cold bottom water. 

​

Around 1:30 PM, I picked up a second monster rockfish, followed by flathead and croaker by-catches. Changing tactics to a slower bottom-contact pause, the rod tip finally fluttered with the unmistakable sharp head-shakes of a **Red Sea Bream (참돔)**! Landing this target snapper made the entire afternoon grind worthwhile.

### 3. Rod Evaluation: AOMEX Navigator Taibarareel Rod

This session marked the inaugural test of the **AOMEX Navigator Taibarareel Rod**.

​

* **Blank:** High-modulus carbon solid tip section paired with a crisp Toray carbon backbone.
* **Performance:** Despite its slim tip diameter, lifting 40cm+ rockfish aboard was smooth with no signs of overload. Sensitivity when feeling subtle nibbles before the hookset was impressive.

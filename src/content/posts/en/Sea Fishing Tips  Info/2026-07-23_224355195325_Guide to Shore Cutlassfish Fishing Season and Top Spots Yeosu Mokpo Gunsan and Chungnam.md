**2026 Season Opening and Regulations**

While recent media reports indicated that the Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries might defer closed-season restrictions for offshore longline commercial fishers, I verified directly with authorities that this exception **does not apply to recreational anglers**. The recreational closed season strictly ends on July 31st at midnight.

​

The 2026 shore cutlassfish season officially opens at 00:00 on August 1st. In early August, inshore sea temperatures above 22°C drive schools of cutlassfish into shallow coastal waters, offering great fishing even for families and beginners.

**[Regulations & Penalties]**
* **Recreational Anglers:** Fines up to 800,000 KRW for possessing undersized fish.
* **Commercial Fishers:** Up to 20 million KRW fine or 2 years imprisonment.
* **Minimum Size Limit:** 18 cm from mouth to anus (Anal Length - applies year-round).

**Regional Breakdown & Key Locations**

1. **Mokpo Region (Yeongam / Geumho / Byeol-am Seawalls):**
   * *Pros:* Yeongam Seawall is the most reliable opener on August 1st. Spots near the Children's Museum, Maritime University, and Shinan Beach Hotel offer convenient urban fishing.
   * *Caution:* Freshwater discharge from Yeongsan River floodgates creates muddy water, dropping bite rates. Check discharge schedules before heading out.

2. **Gunsan / Saemangeum Region (Bieung Port / Yami-do / Seonyu-do):**
   * *Pros:* Great accessibility from the Seoul Metropolitan Area. Well-maintained rest areas (33 Center, Neoul, Dolgurae) along the seawall provide comfortable footholds.
   * *Caution:* High competition for night fishing lights. If your light is weak, consider moving to quieter nearby ports.

3. **Yeosu / South Coast / Busan Region (Dolsan / Wonjeon / Amnam Park):**
   * *Pros:* Dolsan in Yeosu (Impo, Bangjukpo, Soyul, Hwatae Bridge) delivers consistent catch rates all season. Amnam Park in Busan is great for dual-targeting horse mackerel and cuttlefish.
   * *Caution:* Monitor sea temperatures in Busan, as cold water intrusions can temporarily drop activity.

**Tackle & Tactical Essentials**

1. **Rod & Reel Setup:** An Ultra-Light (UL) rockfish/ajing rod, 1000–2000 spinning reel, and 0.4–0.6 PE braided line form the ideal light setup.
2. **Lures:** 1g to 10g reverse jigheads paired with glow-in-the-dark soft plastics (Berkley Baby Sardine or Sandworm). For 1.5-finger fish, 1.5"–2" ajing worms reduce missed strikes.

3. **Gathering Light Strategy:** Cast into the shaded boundary line outside the main light beam, retrieving your lure from dark into light where cutlassfish ambush prey.

4. **Depth Counting:** Count down after casting to locate the active feeding layer. Early in the season, fish feed near the surface (1–3 seconds). As temperatures drop, work deeper layers.

**Field Processing & Storage**

Cutlassfish spoil rapidly. Snip the spine behind the head with shears and pull to remove the head and guts in one movement. Store fish in a cooler iced with 2L frozen water bottles (maintained near 5°C). Lining your cooler with heavy plastic bags keeps it clean and odor-free.

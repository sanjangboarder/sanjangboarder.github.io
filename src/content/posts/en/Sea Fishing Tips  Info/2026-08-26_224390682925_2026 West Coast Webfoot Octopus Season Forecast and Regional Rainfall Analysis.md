### 1. Salinity Impact & Regional Rainfall Data (May–July 2026)

Cephalopods are extremely vulnerable to low salinity caused by heavy freshwater influx into sea bays during their early growth phase. In particular, the **Cheonsu Bay sector in Chungnam** sees the highest impact from freshwater dam discharges, heavily influencing Chungnam's annual catch numbers.

### 3. Port Selection & Opening Day (Sept 1st) Caution

For anglers aiming for record numbers, target major Chungnam ports like **Ocheon Port, Daecheon Port, or Muchangpo**. If charter spots are fully booked, look into smaller ports like **Mageompo, Yeongmok Port, or Daeyado**.

**Opening Day (Sept 1st) Notice:**
Opening day catches on September 1st might temporarily lag behind my high seasonal forecast. This is because Sept 1st lands on an **11-mul spring tide (사리 11물)** with strong currents and muddy water, combined with indirect typhoon swell effects. Catches will likely ramp up significantly entering the first weekend of September. Look at the entire September monthly average rather than judging solely on Sept 1st!

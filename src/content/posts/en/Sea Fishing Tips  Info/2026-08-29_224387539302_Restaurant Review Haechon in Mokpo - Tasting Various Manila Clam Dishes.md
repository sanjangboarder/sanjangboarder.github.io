Today, I am sharing a restaurant review of **Haechon (해촌) in Mokpo**, famous for serving fresh local Manila Clam (바지락) specialties.

---

​

### 1. Manila Clam Hot Pot (Bajirak Jeongol)

When the main dish arrived, my initial skepticism completely evaporated.

​

​

Fresh Manila Clam stock boiled with fresh vegetables... as expected, it was deliciously rich!

​

<div class="single-image">
<img src="https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA4MDhfODIg/MDAxNzg2MTYxMzA1OTU2.yUKhsqxDRiYn4WSxZNPsCUPEvHXrHcAfaEGoX_V91w8g.RvV3n9sAUiR7vl__HEJOQr9hFFDI1GP8jz9Qc145hS4g.JPEG/900_20260808_105322.jpg?type=w800" />
</div>

​

Instead of buying pre-packaged frozen clam meat, Haechon boils fresh whole clams to make the broth and meticulously removes all shell halves by hand, leaving plump, whole clam meat floating in the hot pot. It tastes impeccably fresh.

​

The broth was deeply satisfying. Most impressively, **there was ZERO mud or sand grit**! Paired with mushrooms, water parsley (Minari), and zucchini, the portion size was generous.

My personal dish ranking at Haechon is: **1. Manila Clam Hot Pot -> 2. Porridge -> 3. Bibimbap**. 

Giving it a **4.4 out of 5.0 rating**, Haechon is a restaurant I would proudly bring visiting friends to whenever returning to Mokpo!

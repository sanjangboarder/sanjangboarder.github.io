Last month, while participating in the AOMEX Flatfish Downshot Tournament aboard the *Superman-ho* (part of Team Luffy Fleet out of Daecheonhang Port), I experienced a lunch menu that completely raised my standards for charter boat dining. 

​

Normally, standard boxed lunches (bento) or simple buffet spreads are the norm on Korean fishing charters. However, enjoying freshly grilled pork belly (samgyeopsal) cooked right on an outdoor iron griddle aboard a boat was an absolute game-changer. 

​

I researched charter fleets across Incheon, Chungnam (Namdanghang, Daecheonhang), and Gunsan to compile a list of **West Coast charter boats that take on-board dining to a whole new level with live iron-griddle cooking**.

​

---

### 1. Incheon Area Charters

While Incheon fleet setups are generally more compact, two well-known boats feature griddle cooking equipment:

​

#### Supernova-ho (Incheon South Port / 남항유어선부두)
* **Location:** 142 Chukhang-daero, Jemuolpo-gu, Incheon
* **Menu Special:** Iron-Griddle Stir-Fried Pork (Duruchigi / 두루치기).
* **Overview:** Extremely popular among veteran Incheon anglers, making reservations tough to secure. Serving freshly cooked, seasoned pork on the open deck sets it apart from standard boxed-lunch charter options.

#### Lukina-ho (Incheon Yeonan Pier / 연안부두)
* **Menu Special:** Iron-Griddle Spicy Stir-Fried Chicken (Dak-galbi / 닭갈비).
* **Overview:** Equipped with dedicated griddle stations. During extreme mid-summer heatwaves, they occasionally switch to premium catered bento boxes for food safety, but their specialty remains live griddle cooking.

### 2. Chungnam Area Charters (Hongseong & Boryeong)

​

#### Eungabi-ho (Namdanghang Port / 남항당)
* **Location:** Namdang-ri, Seobu-myeon, Hongseong-gun, Chungcheongnam-do
* **Menu Special:** Iron-Griddle Sliced Pork Belly with Aged Kimchi.
* **Overview:** Sizzling thick cuts of pork belly right on the deck, frying aged kimchi in the rendered pork fat (lard)—a feast that anglers rave about during full-day offshore trips.

​

​

#### Team Luffy Fleet: Luffy-ho, Team Luffy-ho, & Superman-ho (Daecheonhang Port / 대천항)
* **Location:** 1250 Sinheuk-dong, Boryeong-si, Chungcheongnam-do
* **Menu Special:** Premium Pork Belly + Sausages + Kimchi + **Finish Flying-Fish Roe Fried Rice**.
* **Overview:** Hands down the gold standard of charter food on the West Coast. Not only do they sizzle pork belly and sausages, but deckhands also toss in flying fish roe, cheese, and rice to create ultimate fried rice at the end of the meal.

### Conclusion

If you want your saltwater fishing outings to double as a memorable culinary experience, booking a session on one of these griddle-dining charters is an unforgettable treat. Food menus may adjust depending on seasonality and heatwaves, but as of mid-2026, Namdanghang's *Eungabi-ho* and Daecheonhang's *Team Luffy Fleet* remain top recommendations!

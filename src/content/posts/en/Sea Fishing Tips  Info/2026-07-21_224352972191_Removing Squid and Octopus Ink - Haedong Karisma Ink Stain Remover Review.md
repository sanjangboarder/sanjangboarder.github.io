I enjoy various types of cephalopod fishing—including webfoot octopus, cuttlefish, swordtip squid, neon flying squid, and giant octopus. While cephalopod fishing guarantees non-stop action, there is one major hazard anglers must deal with: **squid and octopus ink**.

​

Ink squirted by large spring cuttlefish or octopus can stain clothes so severely that many anglers end up throwing their shirts away. For a long time, I viewed ink stains as badges of honor and didn't bother removing them. However, after accumulating 2 to 3 years of ink splatters on one of my favorite summer fishing shirts, it reached a point where it looked a bit too rough. So, I decided to acquire the **Haedong Karisma Ink Remover** designed specifically for cleaning cephalopod ink and organic stains.

**Results Summary:**
* **Socks:** Near-perfect ink removal. Since the ink stains were relatively recent (from spring cuttlefish trips), they came out completely.
* **Arm Sleeves:** Stains faded significantly, rendering them almost unnoticeable.
* **Short-sleeve Shirt:** Because ink had set in for over 3 years, faint ghost marks remain under close inspection, but from a normal distance, the shirt looks clean and presentable.

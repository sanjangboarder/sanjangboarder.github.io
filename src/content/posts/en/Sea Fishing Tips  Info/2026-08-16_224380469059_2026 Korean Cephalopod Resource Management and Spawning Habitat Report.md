### 1. Regional Fry Release Statistics (2023–2026 Comparison)

Fry release initiatives restocking juvenile octopus and cuttlefish have expanded significantly along the West Coast:

​

#### Jeonbuk & Gunsan Sector
* **2026 Releases:** 350,000 Webfoot Octopus + **400,000 Cuttlefish** (Record High Cuttlefish Release!).
* **Trend:** Cuttlefish releases increased by over 230% compared to 2025 (170,000), signaling a major stock recovery push for Gunsan waters.

​

#### Jeonnam Sector
* **2026 Releases:** 400,000 Webfoot Octopus + 23,000 Cuttlefish.

### 2. Spawning Habitat Shelter Installations (1.2 Million+ Units)

Even more promising than fry releases is the deployment of **natural shell and concrete egg-nesting shelters** along coastal reefs:

​

| Province / Region | Target Sea Areas | Spawning Shelters Deployed | Key Project Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incheon Metro** | Ongjin-gun (Ijak-do, Seungbong-do) | **500,000 Units** | Finalizing 300-hectare concentrated octopus breeding ground. |
| **Chungnam Province** | Taean Coastal (Anmyeon-do, Nam-myeon) | **410,000 Units** | Multi-year government project with 3 Billion KRW budget through 2030. |
| **Chungnam Province** | Boryeong Coastal (Daecheonhang) | **100,000 Units** | Managed jointly with local fishermen associations. |
| **Gyeonggi Province** | Ansan Coastal Reefs | **120,000 Units** | Eco-friendly shell habitat structures fixed along seabed reefs. |
| **Jeonbuk Province** | Gochang Coastal (Gusipo) | **88,000 Units** | Long-term natural spawning induction project. |
| **Jeonbuk Province** | Gunsan Coastal (Yeon-do, Bian-do) | **50,000 Units** | Anti-drift structural placement with ecological monitoring. |

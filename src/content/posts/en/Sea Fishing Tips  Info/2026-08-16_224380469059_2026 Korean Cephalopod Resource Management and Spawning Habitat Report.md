### 1. Regional Restocking Statistics (2023 – 2026 Comparison)

​

#### Jeonbuk & Gunsan Sector
Most Jeonbuk cephalopod fishing points are centered around Gunsan. Following a terrible yield two years ago and a mediocre season last year, Gunsan's appeal as a top squid destination had somewhat dipped. This year, local organizations actively protested to protect natural octopus spawning grounds and established additional breeding zones.

​

| Year | Webfoot Octopus Releases | Cuttlefish Releases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2026** | **350,000** | **400,000** |
| **2025** | 250,000 | 170,000 |
| **2024** | 350,000 | - |
| **2023** | 70,000 | - |

​

The most striking figure is the massive spike in cuttlefish releases—up to **400,000 fry** in 2026 compared to 170,000 in 2025. It will be very interesting to see if this surge elevates Gunsan into a premier cuttlefish hot spot next season. Primary releases took place off Buan, Gunsan, and Gochang.

### 2. Spawning Habitat Shelter Deploys (1.2 Million+ Structures)

While 2026 fry releases remained fairly consistent with previous years, the **massive expansion of spawning shelter installations** across West Coast sea beds is the single most exciting development this year!

​

Combined, Incheon and Gyeonggi province received approximately **620,000 spawning shelter units**. Personally, I hold higher expectations for these artificial nesting shelters than for simple fry releases. If Incheon outpaces Chungnam waters in total catch yields over the next two seasons, these spawning shelter investments will be the primary driving factor.

​

| Province / Region | Target Sea Areas | Spawning Shelters Deployed | Key Project Scope & Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incheon Metro** | Ongjin-gun (Ijak-do, Seungbong-do) | **500,000 Units** | Finalizing 300-hectare concentrated octopus breeding ground through 2026. |
| **Chungnam Province** | Taean Coastal (Geunheung, Anmyeon-do) | **410,000 Units** | Ministry of Oceans national project with 3 Billion KRW budget adding 250k+ units annually through 2030. |
| **Chungnam Province** | Boryeong Coastal (Daecheonhang) | **100,000 Units** | Managed jointly with local fishermen associations. |
| **Gyeonggi Province** | Ansan Coastal Reefs | **120,000 Units** | Eco-friendly shell habitat structures fixed along seabed reefs. |
| **Jeonbuk Province** | Gochang Coastal (Gusipo) | **88,000 Units** | 3 Billion KRW long-term project inducing natural spawning through 2030. |
| **Jeonbuk Province** | Gunsan Coastal (Yeon-do, Bian-do) | **50,000 Units** | Anti-drift structural placement with ecological monitoring. |

​

Chungnam received the second-largest share at **510,000 shelter units** (410,000 in Taean + 100,000 in Boryeong). Historically, Anmyeon-do in Taean was not considered a prime octopus destination; it will be fascinating to see if these new spawning beds turn it into a top-tier fishing zone.

### 2. Soaking & Application Steps

1. **Warm Water Soaking:** Prepared 5 liters of warm water in a washbasin and fully submerged all three garments.
2. **Initial Dosage:** Added 3 scoops of the ink remover powder and dropped 10 drops of the protein remover liquid.
3. **Soaking Period:** Let it soak for 3 hours. Since the t-shirt had deep, 3-year-old ink stains, I added an extra 2 scoops of powder and 5 drops of liquid, extending the total soak time to 5 hours.

* **Socks:** Near-perfect ink removal! The recent spring cuttlefish stains disappeared almost entirely.

### Summary & Tip

If you treat squid ink stains **immediately after returning home from a trip**, you will achieve near-perfect results with much smaller amounts of solution. For old, set-in stains, a two-stage soaking process yields the best restoration.

Located in Incheon, **Muui-do Island** is one of the most accessible and popular saltwater shore fishing destinations in the Seoul Metropolitan Area. Among its top spots, **Deokjeom Breakwater** and its adjacent rocky shoreline offer great opportunities for lure, float, and surf casting anglers. Here is an in-depth breakdown of how to tackle these spots effectively.

### 1. Deokjeom Breakwater

​

#### Underwater Structure & Snag Prevention
The water directly in front of the breakwater stone embankment features an underwater ledge. If you retrieve your lure all the way to your feet along the bottom, your rig will almost certainly snag on the ledge. 

​

* **Pro Tip:** Use multicolor PE braided line (with 10-meter color increments). Note the specific color band where your rig reaches the nearshore ledge and rapidly reel in to clear the underwater shelf!

​

#### Tidal Currents & Etiquette
Deokjeom Breakwater acts as a natural water channel, producing strong side currents during both ebb and flood tides. 

​

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA3MDRfMjky/MDAxNzgzMTIxNzI2NTE3.ctuVFgKsHzk23yuKR6yVgJ2i5pTHkNsmrLFAPy7E9_Qg.-_I3FLTp8vYx6dxqNxH3aZAvnQfMdLD2Csbjirl8LRIg.PNG/image.png?type=w800)

​

Because lure, float, and surf anglers fish side by side, pay close attention to line drift. Letting heavy sinkers roll endlessly or allowing floats to drift across neighboring anglers' lanes will lead to line tangles. Communication and courtesy go a long way here.

​

#### Target Species
* **Rockfish, Greenling, Flatfish, Spanish Mackerel, Cutlassfish, Webfoot Octopus.**

### 2. Deokjeom Rocky Shore Point (덕점 갯바위)

​

#### Access & Safe Tide Windows
Access to the rocky shore is strictly tide-dependent. You can walk around the rocky point starting from mid-ebb tide, but you **must leave the area before mid-flood tide** to avoid getting cut off by incoming water.

​

#### Bottom Profile & Casting Distance
The rock/boulder reef extends outwards over 40 meters from the high-tide line. During high tide, bottom-dwelling fish require long casting distances (over 80–100 meters) to clear the boulder field.

​

* **Low Tide Strategy:** The optimal window is around low tide. As the water recedes past the boulder edge, short casting distance is no longer a handicap, allowing beginners to reach deep water.

### Conclusion

While Muui-do is beginner-friendly in terms of travel distance, landing fish consistently requires understanding tide cycles and casting over bottom ledges. Targeting the low-tide window will yield the best results for flatfish and rockfish.

Non-fishing acquaintances often ask me: *"If you go boat fishing all the time and catch that much fish, doesn't it save you a ton of money compared to buying fish at the market?"* Haha. Whenever I hear this question, I always answer with a firm **NO**.

​

Today, purely out of fun, I have calculated and put together a detailed cost-comparison breakdown: **Charter Boat Fishing Expenses vs. Fresh Seafood Market Prices** across popular species.

---

​

### 1. White Croaker (백조기) Boat Fishing

White Croaker is famous for filling coolers with high catch numbers on West Coast boat charters.

* **Charter Trip Cost:** Boat fee ~80,000 KRW + Transportation ~30,000 to 50,000 KRW + Filleting/salting fee (2.5k KRW/kg x ~10kg to 16kg = ~25,000 to 40,000 KRW) = **Total ~130,000 to 150,000 KRW**.
* **Market Buying Cost:** Buying semi-dried White Croaker online (equivalent to ~6.4kg fresh weight) costs about **80,000 KRW**.
* **Result:** **Buying at the market is 50,000 to 70,000 KRW CHEAPER.**

### 2. Inshore Rockfish (내만권 우럭) Boat Fishing

Assuming a generous catch of three 40cm+ rockfish or ten 30cm+ rockfish totaling 3kg of raw fish:

* **Charter Trip Cost:** Boat fee ~100,000 KRW + Transportation ~30,000 KRW + Filleting fee ~15,000 KRW = **Total ~140,000 KRW**.
* **Market Buying Cost:** Purchasing 3kg of live rockfish at fish markets (~40,000 KRW/kg) = **Total ~120,000 KRW**.
* **Result:** **Buying at the market is ~20,000 KRW CHEAPER.**

### 3. Large Flounder (대광어) Boat Fishing

May to June is peak Large Flounder season on the West Coast. Assuming a monster 6kg wild flounder is landed:

* **Charter Trip Cost:** Boat fee ~110,000 KRW + Transportation ~30,000 KRW + Filleting fee ~30,000 KRW = **Total ~170,000 KRW**.
* **Market Buying Cost:** In May/June, wild flounder prices drop to 20,000~30,000 KRW/kg. Buying 6kg at the market costs **~120,000 to 150,000 KRW**. (And remember, skunking out completely on flounder boats is very common!).
* **Result:** **Buying at the market is 20,000 to 50,000 KRW CHEAPER.**

### 4. Red Seabream (참돔) Boat Fishing

Assuming a generous average catch of 2 large fish totaling 3kg:

* **Charter Trip Cost:** Boat fee ~110,000 KRW + Transportation ~30,000 KRW + Filleting fee ~15,000 KRW = **Total ~150,000 KRW**.
* **Market Buying Cost:** Farmed Red Seabream runs about 40,000 KRW/kg. Buying 3kg costs **~120,000 KRW**.
* **Result:** **Buying at the market is ~30,000 KRW CHEAPER.**

### 5. Spring Cuttlefish (봄 갑오징어) Boat Fishing

Targeting jumbo "rugby-ball" cuttlefish from May to July, with an average catch of 2 big cuttlefish (2kg total):

* **Charter Trip Cost:** Boat fee ~80,000 KRW + Transportation ~30,000 KRW + Filleting fee ~10,000 KRW = **Total ~120,000 KRW**.
* **Market Buying Cost:** Fresh cuttlefish with sashimi filleting service costs 40,000~50,000 KRW/kg. 2kg costs **~100,000 KRW**.
* **Result:** **Buying at the market is ~20,000 KRW CHEAPER.**

### 6. Webfoot Octopus (주꾸미) & Night Mackerel (야간 고등어)

* **Webfoot Octopus (Autumn):** During peak autumn season (Sept-Oct), commercial catches are minimal while angler catches dominate the market. Autumn octopus is one of the rare exceptions where catching your own on a boat actually holds economic advantage! (Though during spring egg-bearing season in April-May, buying at the market is still cheaper!).
* **Night Mackerel:** Charter fees (~130k KRW) + gas (~30k KRW) + filleting (~50k KRW) = **210,000 KRW total**. Purchasing 12kg of fresh Jeju mackerel costs only **~80,000 KRW**. Buying is **130,000 KRW CHEAPER!**

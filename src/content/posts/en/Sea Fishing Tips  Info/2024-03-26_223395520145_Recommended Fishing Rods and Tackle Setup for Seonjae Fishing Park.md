Key target species currently stocked include:
* **Red Sea Bream (참돔):** High-fighting fish requiring strong backbone power.
* **Rockfish (우럭):** Active bottom feeders near drop-offs and structure.
* **Sea Bass (농어):** Fast swimmers requiring smooth reel drag and long-cast ability.
* **Flatfish (광어):** Hiding on sandy bottoms, sensitive to subtle lure presentations.

### 2. Recommended Fishing Rod Specifications

Saltwater fishing parks enforce specific rod length limits to ensure safety and prevent line tangles among anglers.

​

* **Rod Length:** 1.8m to 2.1m (6.0ft to 7.0ft) max length recommended.
* **Rod Power:** Medium-Light (ML) to Medium (M) power.
* **Action:** Fast to Extra-Fast tip action for precise lure control and quick hooksets.

A Medium-Light (ML) rod strikes the ideal balance between casting light float rigs/jigheads and possessing enough butt strength to turn powerful Red Sea Breams away from underwater ropes and pilings.

### 3. Reel, Line & Terminal Rig Setup

* **Spinning Reel:** Size 2500 to C3000 with a reliable drag system.
* **Main Line:** 8-strand PE Braided Line #1.0 to #1.5 (approx. 15lb to 20lb test).
* **Shock Leader:** Fluorocarbon 12lb to 16lb (3.0# to 4.0#).
* **Float Rigs & Jigheads:** 0.5 to 1.5 unsewn floats, 1/4oz to 1/2oz jigheads paired with 3 to 4-inch soft plastic worms or live shrimp.

### 4. Key Tactics for Success at Seonjae Park

1. **Targeting Water Intake & Outflow Points:** Oxygen-rich currents near pumps draw active sea bass and red breams.
2. **Structure Management:** When hooked, apply firm upward rod pressure immediately to prevent big fish from taking cover under dock posts.
3. **Bait Selection:** Alternate between live shrimp for red sea bream and paddle-tail soft worms for sea bass and rockfish based on time of day.

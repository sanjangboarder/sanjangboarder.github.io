### 2. Crucial Upgrade: 130mm Ultra-Lightweight Double Handle Tuning

Factory stock handles on BFS reels often feature small, uncomfortable knobs or short 90mm arms that make pulling heavy sinkers exhausting.

​

* **Why Upgrade to 130mm?** Extending the handle torque arm to 130mm increases rotational leverage, reducing cranking effort by over 30%.
* **KF ADH Air 130mm Carbon Handle:** Weighing just **19g**, upgrading to this carbon handle maintains sub-130g total reel weight while providing superior ergonomic grip.

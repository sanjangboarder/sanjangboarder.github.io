### 2. Major Tackle Manufacturers & Booth Highlights

With the autumn Webfoot Octopus (주꾸미) and Cuttlefish (갑오징어) season launching on September 1st, several top Korean tackle makers are showcasing new gear:

​

* **AOMEX (아오맥스):** Displaying their 2026 lineup of ultra-lightweight Tenbin, Octopus, and Squid rods.
* **Pro Fishing (Daiwa Distributor):** Offering 10% to 15% on-site discounts on Daiwa reels, apparel, and accessories.
* **Yujeong Fishing & OSLO:** Featuring budget-friendly lightweight inshore rods.
* **EGI_TEC & Bighit Fishing:** Selling high-reflectivity laser squid jigs (Egi) below online retail prices.

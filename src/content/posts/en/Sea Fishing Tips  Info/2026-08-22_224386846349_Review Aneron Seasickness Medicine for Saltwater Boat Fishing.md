### 1. Why Aneron for Boat Fishing?

While liquid anti-nausea syrups or domestic pills work fine on calm waters, heavy offshore swells demand a stronger preventative solution.

​

* **Single Daily Capsule:** Taking just **1 capsule 30 minutes before boarding** provides long-lasting protection against dizziness, nausea, and cold sweats throughout a full-day charter.
* **Preemptive Use:** Rather than waiting for symptoms to kick in on a choppy sea, taking one capsule preemptively ensures zero downtime on deck.

### 2. Direct Import Customs Rules & Shelf Life

When ordering Aneron directly from Japanese cross-border sites, keep these key rules in mind:

​

* **Customs Duty Limit:** Total order value (product price + shipping) must remain below $150 USD.
* **Maximum Import Limit:** Korean customs restricts personal medication imports to a **maximum of 6 boxes per shipment**. Any boxes exceeding 6 will be confiscated and disposed of at customs.
* **Shelf Life:** Expiration dates average around 2 years. Ordering 2 to 3 boxes per year is optimal to avoid expired stock.

### 3. Side Effect Caution: Driving Fatigue

* **Drowsiness Warning:** Like many effective antihistamine-based motion sickness remedies, Aneron can induce mild drowsiness once off the boat.
* **Safety Tip:** Always rest properly at harbor cafes or highway service areas before driving home after a full day on the water.

For anglers serious about offshore charter trips, carrying 1 or 2 capsules of Aneron in your tackle box is the best insurance against ruined fishing days.

**Key Gear Checklist:**
1. **Light Lure Rod:** 7.6ft–8.3ft UL/L spinning rod for casting 3g–10g jigheads.
2. **Reverse Jigheads & Soft Baits:** 3g to 7g reverse-hook jigheads with 2-inch glow shad worms.
3. **Wire Leaders:** Short 10cm wire or thick fluorocarbon shock leaders to prevent teeth cut-offs.

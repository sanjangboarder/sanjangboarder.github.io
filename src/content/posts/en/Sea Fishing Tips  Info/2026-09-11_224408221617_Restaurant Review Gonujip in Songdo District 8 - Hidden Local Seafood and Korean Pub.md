Today, I am reviewing a hidden neighborhood tavern frequented by local regulars in **Songdo District 8, Incheon: Gonujip**, known for its creative seasonal Korean drinking snacks and fresh seafood platters.

---

​

### 1. Complimentary Welcome Appetizers

The moment you sit down, an impressive spread of complimentary welcome dishes arrives: spicy shredded radish, cucumber mentaiko mayonnaise salad, boiled sea snails (Godung), and a piping hot bowl of beef radish soup (Sogogi Muguk). These service appetizers rotate seasonally depending on market ingredients.

​

### Conclusion & Rating

**Rating: 4.2 / 5.0**

Gonujip stands out for its distinctive menu curation, fresh seafood, and generous welcome dishes. The only minor drawback is its residential apartment arcade location, which is slightly secluded for visitors from outside Songdo. However, customer parking is provided. It regularly packs to full capacity on weeknights, so calling ahead for reservations is strongly recommended.

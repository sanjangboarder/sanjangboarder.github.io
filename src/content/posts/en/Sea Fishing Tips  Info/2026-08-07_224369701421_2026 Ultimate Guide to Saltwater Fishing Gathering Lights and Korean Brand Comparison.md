### 1. Key Technical Specifications to Understand Before Buying

* **Battery Capacity Math (Watt-Hours Wh):** Always check total Watt-hours ($Wh = Voltage \times Amp-Hours$). Modern 14.8V 4S Li-ion battery banks typically range from 4S10P (700Wh+) to 4S14P (1000Wh+).
* **LED Head Wattage vs. Runtime:** A 100W LED head paired with an 850Wh battery bank provides roughly 8.5 hours of continuous maximum illumination.
* **Power Delivery (PD) Fast Charging:** 2026 generation power packs feature 100W USB-C PD input/output, allowing fast recharge cycles and mobile device charging.

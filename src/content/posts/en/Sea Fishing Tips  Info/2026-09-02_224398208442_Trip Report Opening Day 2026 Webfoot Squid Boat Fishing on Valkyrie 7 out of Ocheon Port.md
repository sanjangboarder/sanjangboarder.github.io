​

As action slowed, our captain shifted toward small island spots past Yeongmok Port, where larger-sized squid picked up steadily. Right before a late lunch, my tally stood at **120 Webfoot Squid and 11 Cuttlefish**. Considering opening day fell on a late-spring tide with freshwater runoff, hitting 200 would have been great, but conditions proved tougher than expected.

​

### 2. Afternoon Session & Final Catch Result

Post-lunch, we worked spots outside Won-san Bridge and between Yeongmok and Boryeong ports. Bite frequency oscillated up and down depending on tide shifts.

​

Afternoon conditions grew harsher as wind picked up, lowering tactile sensitivity while bright sun broke through morning rain.

Comparing against previous years, catches were reasonable given tide and freshwater variables. Notably, cuttlefish aggressively struck egi lures from opening day, suggesting Chungnam cuttlefish catches will explode entering October.

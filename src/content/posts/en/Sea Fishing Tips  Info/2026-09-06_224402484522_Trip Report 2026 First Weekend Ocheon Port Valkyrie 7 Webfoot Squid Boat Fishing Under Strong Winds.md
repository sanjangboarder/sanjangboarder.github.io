### 1. Morning Sector & Spot Shifts Near Boryeong Power Plant

Around lunchtime, our captain moved the vessel toward deeper spots near the Boryeong Power Plant harbor. Scores of charter boats were clustered in this area. While the water depth was noticeably deeper, the average size of the webfoot squid was significantly larger. Bites weren't a non-stop frenzy ("Neuna-neuna"), but steadily trickled in one by one.

​

### 2. Afternoon Surge & Final Count

By 2:00 PM, checking my tally counter showed **163 Webfoot Squid and 6 Cuttlefish**. While not a historic blowout, reaching my daily target of 200 fish felt completely achievable.

​

During the afternoon session, **Watermelon and Green color egi lures** triggered the strongest bites. While small-sized squid appeared mid-afternoon, our final spot produced thick, heavy-class squid. Although bite speed slowed compared to morning, I steadily stacked fish one by one.

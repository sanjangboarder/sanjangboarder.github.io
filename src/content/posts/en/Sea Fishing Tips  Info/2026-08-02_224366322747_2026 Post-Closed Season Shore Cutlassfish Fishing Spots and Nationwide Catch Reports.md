August 1st marks the official lifting of the annual **Cutlassfish Closed Fishing Season** in Korea! With night temperatures warming up, thousands of saltwater anglers are heading to breakwaters and sea walls for juvenile cutlassfish (풀치 / fingerling cutlassfish). Here is a nationwide region-by-region roundup of early August catch reports and top recommended spots.

### 1. Namhae Region (South Coast) — **HOTTEST SPOT**

Namhae Island currently leads the nation with explosive early-season action. 

​

* **Catch Rate:** Massive schools of 1.5 to 2.5 finger-width cutlassfish have moved into breakwaters.
* **Top Spots:** Mulgeon Breakwater (물건방파제) and Gain-ri shorelines. Anglers casting light jighead rigs are landing dozens of fish per night session.

​

---

### 2. Yeosu Region (Dolsan Area)

Yeosu is producing impressive size profile for early August, with solid 2.5-finger cutlassfish being reported.

​

* **Top Spots:** Jinmok Breakwater (진목방파제) on Dolsan Island and Bangjukpo shorelines. While spot selection matters, those targeting deep water channels under streetlights are catching solid numbers.

### 3. Mokpo & Haenam Region

The West-South coastal sea walls are living up to their reputation for consistent numbers.

​

* **Top Spot:** Yeongam Geumho Seawall (영암금호방조제). 
* **Action:** Early August runs show steady catches of 1.5 to 2.0 finger-width cutlassfish. Light micro-jigheads with glow worms are producing steady bites.

### 4. Gunsan Saemangeum & Busan/Geoje (Slower Early Start)

* **Gunsan Saemangeum Seawall:** Action is still slow for early August. It is best to wait until mid-to-late August for schools to move into the inner seawall.
* **Busan & Geoje:** Locations like Yeongdo Breakwaters have yielded low early-season catches. Expect numbers to improve as coastal water temperatures rise toward mid-August.

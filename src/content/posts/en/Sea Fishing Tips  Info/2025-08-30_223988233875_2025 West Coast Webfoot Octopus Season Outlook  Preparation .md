**Key Season Factors:**
1. **Water Temperature & Tides:** Early September neap tides (*Jogeum*) offer optimal current speeds for bottom jigging around Boryeong and Taean.
2. **Egi Lure Trends:** Laser-finish 60mm–70mm mini egis and glow watermelon colors are expected to dominate early-season bites.
3. **Tackle Choice:** Sub-50g rods paired with 100g-class baitcasting reels provide maximum sensitivity for detecting light octopus weight additions.

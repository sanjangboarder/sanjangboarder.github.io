### 1. Essential Gear & Tackle Setup

* **Rods:** Light Jigging, Taibarareel, or Ika Metal rods with flexible tips capable of working 60g to 100g metal jigs.
* **Reels:** **Digital Line-Counter Baitcasting Reels** are strongly recommended. Mackerel schools suspend at specific depth bands (e.g., 18 meters), so precise depth matching across anglers is vital.
* **Main Line:** #1.0 to #1.5 PE braided line paired with a 16lb to 20lb shock leader to handle sudden Spanish Mackerel strikes.

### 2. Rigs, Live Bait & Safety Tools

1. **Heavy-Duty Sabiki Rigs:** Avoid weak 10-hook inshore sabiki rigs that snap under multiple 35cm+ mackerel. Use 2 to 3-hook heavy-duty dropper rigs with short branch lines.
2. **Fresh Cut Bait Tip:** Tipping metal jig hooks with fresh slices of small mackerel (Godori) caught on deck produces exponentially higher strike rates than artificial lures alone.
3. **Safety Accessories (Forceps & Fish Gripper):** **Never unhook thrashing mackerel by hand!** Multiple treble hooks swinging on frantic fish can easily impale fingers. Always hold fish with heavy-duty fish grippers and unhook using long surgical forceps.

### 3. Depth-Finding Tactics & Teamwork

Mackerel schools shift depth rapidly beneath boat gathering lights.

​

* **Locating the Suspended Layer:** Drop metal jigs to the bottom and perform quick short-jerks combined with 3-second tensioned pauses on the retrieve.
* **Depth Sharing:** Once an angler hookup occurs, immediately call out the exact digital counter depth (e.g., "16 meters!"). When all anglers drop to the same depth, the feeding frenzy keeps the school pinned beneath the vessel.

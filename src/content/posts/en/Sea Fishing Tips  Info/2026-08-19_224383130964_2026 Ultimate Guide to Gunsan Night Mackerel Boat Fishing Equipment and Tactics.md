### 1. Choosing the Right Trip Dates & Tides

For Gunsan night mackerel charters, timing is everything. Look for stable water temperatures right after spring tides (사리 물때) during July through September. Checking up-to-the-minute daily charter catch logs is vital before booking your spot.

​

---

### 2. Rod & Reel Setup

* **Rods:** Flexible Light Jigging, Taibarareel, or Ika Metal rods with soft tips capable of handling 60g to 100g metal jigs without bouncing fish off the hook.
* **Reels:** **Digital Line-Counter Baitcasting Reels** are absolutely essential. Mackerel schools suspend at precise depth layers (e.g., 16 meters), so knowing your exact depth line is critical.
* **Main Line:** #1.0 to #1.5 PE braided line paired with a 16lb to 20lb shock leader to withstand sharp Spanish Mackerel teeth.

​

---

### 3. Bait, Terminal Rigs & Accessories

​

#### Fresh Cut Bait & Filleting Knife
While store-bought saury strips or squid strips work fine, cutting fresh bait fillets on deck from small juvenile mackerel (called *Godori*) is by far the most effective bait. Bring a sharp filleting knife to prepare fresh bait strips on board.

​

#### Multi-Hook Dropper Rigs (Avoid Weak Inshore Sabikis!)
When using live bait alongside metal jigs, run a multi-hook dropper rig. **Never use cheap 5 to 10-hook inshore sabiki rigs!** Those weak rigs are designed for shore casting; under the strain of multiple 35cm+ mackerel, they snap line, tangle neighbors, and cause chaos on deck. Instead, use heavy-duty 2 to 3-hook rigs built with thick trunk lines and short branch lines to prevent tangles.

​

#### Underwater Attraction Lights
Attaching an eyeball-shaped underwater LED light near the metal jig head or an inline squid light on the leader noticeably increases strike frequency.

#### Cooler Size Limits
While everyone dreams of filling a massive cooler, an average good day yields enough to fill a 24-liter to 37-liter cooler. Bringing giant 50L+ coolers congests walking aisles on deck. A 37-liter long-style cooler (like the Haedong Karisma 37L) is ideal for laying long Spanish Mackerel flat without crowding the boat.

​

#### Safety Essentials: Long Forceps & Thick Fish Grippers
**NEVER unhook thrashing mackerel or toothy Spanish Mackerel by hand!** Multiple treble hooks swinging wildly on thrashing fish cause frequent severe hand injuries on night boats. Knock Spanish Mackerel unconscious immediately, hold mackerel firmly using thick-jaw fish grippers, and extract hooks using long surgical forceps.

### 4. Tactical Depth-Finding & Boat Teamwork

70% of success in night mackerel fishing comes down to finding and holding the active feeding depth band. 

​

* **Calling Out Depths:** Mackerel move constantly beneath boat lights. Once an angler hooked up at 18 meters, call out "18 meters!" so everyone drops to the exact same depth counter reading. This teamwork keeps the feeding frenzy pinned beneath the boat.
* **Retrieval Actions:** Rotate between sharp short-jerks and controlled 3-second tensioned falls. Keep an eye on anglers catching consistently to copy their jig colors, weights, and retrieve cadences.

Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

Since switching my phone to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 about a month ago, I have been replacing my existing wireless chargers with MagSafe chargers one by one. Having previously used a Bendict car MagSafe wireless charger with great satisfaction and even recommending it, this time I was looking for a 3-in-1 desktop charger to place on my desk that could simultaneously charge my Fold 8, Galaxy Watch, and wireless earbuds all at once. It turned out Bendict had the exact product I needed.

The product name is the Bendict Luna Aero Station. Bendict's packaging and delivery speed are always satisfying; it arrived clean, neat, and securely packaged.

The package contents of the Bendict Luna Aero Station are very straightforward: it includes just a USB-C cable and the main MagSafe wireless charger stand.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMDVfMjQy/MDAxNzkxMTgzMzczNDM3.d7TiZl5xXVYQ7_iJsYNswHdsyuPpg3SWzVuK195oyEAg.u8fAkJumCEIpEKICx1c97Sg0cblq9DpgKM3OE8C4CRUg.JPEG/SE-8103c92a-bfe9-11f1-8edd-eb57aebcd1f1.jpg?type=w800)

Because the Luna Aero Station supports 25W ultra-fast wireless charging—an enhancement beyond the traditional 15W Qi2 standard—a USB cable that supports ultra-fast wireless charging is necessary. Fortunately, one is included in the box.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMDRfMTQg/MDAxNzkxMTE0NTMwOTQ0.QdR0toxFupeRVBkpllWwY1q6gq4LgQkyFvD7i48RFVIg.b-_BGWpHrlrjD9Tw7-S99VdVIyZWzGrJjvS99jjgF0Ig.JPEG/900_20261004_185900.jpg?type=w800)

It features a 3-in-1 multi-charging system that simultaneously charges a smartphone, smartwatch, and Bluetooth earbuds, with all three dedicated spots supporting wireless charging. As shown in the image below, the smartphone MagSafe charger is the section labeled Qi2, while the black base area serves as the wireless charger for Bluetooth earbuds.

The design adopts a minimalist aesthetic that eliminates all unnecessary clutter, finished in classic silver with tasteful accent colors, making it easy to recommend to anyone who appreciates sophisticated styling.

In recent wireless charger reviews, a common issue often raised is thermal throttling, where heat generated during wireless charging causes devices like the Fold 8 to heat up and throttle charging speeds. The Bendict Luna Aero Station incorporates Aero Turbine cooling technology designed to maintain stable operation without speed drops even at maximum output.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjEwMDRfNTIg/MDAxNzkxMTE0NTI5Nzcx.nI5HJ5arQPwxXtXCUM1TvyuoqKer7oRwlTuH-2ra_h4g.8T6jgP-mOzx4khPfPWl6u_aATmPwa2Tq3_sgZFwwwEsg.JPEG/900_20261004_185925.jpg?type=w800)

When reading user reviews of cooled MagSafe chargers, I often saw complaints about loud fan noise being distracting. Fortunately, when I tested it myself, the fan noise during cooling operation was barely noticeable, keeping it very quiet whether placed on an office desk or at home. This makes it a great selling point for anyone who values a quiet environment.

In addition, the MagSafe wireless charging pad features a tilting mechanism adjustable up to 35 degrees, allowing you to angle the screen precisely to your preferred viewing angle.

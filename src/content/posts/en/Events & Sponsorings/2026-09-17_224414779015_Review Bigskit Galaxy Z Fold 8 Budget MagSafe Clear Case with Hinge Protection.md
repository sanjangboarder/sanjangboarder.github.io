Hello, this is SanjangBorder.

​

It has been about a month since I started actively using the Galaxy Z Fold 8. I have already tested two carbon fiber cases, but the big drawback of carbon cases was that they completely covered up the stunning Pistachio color I specifically purchased my Galaxy Z Fold 8 in, masking it behind solid black.

​

Therefore, I decided to pick up a transparent clear case for the Fold 8 that allows the Pistachio color to shine through. My selection criteria for the case were: first, it must provide hinge protection; second, it must support MagSafe; and third, it must be made of a clear material resistant to yellowing and discoloration. On top of that, having great cost-performance would make it the ideal choice.

​

The product I chose based on those requirements is the Bigskit Galaxy Z Fold 8 Case. The package arrived quickly and securely, as shown in the photos below.

First is the front case piece. The standout feature you immediately notice is that it is engineered so you do not need a separate front screen protector film. With Fold 8 cases—as with earlier Galaxy Fold models—because the front screen-to-body ratio is so high (the entire front is practically display), front frames often have to overlap the edges of the screen, making it notoriously tricky to attach aftermarket screen protectors without bubbling or lifting.

​

The hinge cover is designed to sit flush and snug when the device is folded, and smoothly glides along an articulated track when unfolded.

​

Another indispensable feature when using the Fold is MagSafe compatibility. Due to the Fold 8's unique form factor and coil placement, standard Qi wireless chargers designed for flat smartphones—whether desk pads or car mounts—rarely align properly. MagSafe is therefore essential for hassle-free wireless charging. The Bigskit case naturally includes built-in MagSafe rings, and the magnetic pull with my MagSafe chargers and grip toks felt remarkably strong and secure.

A dedicated, state-of-the-art **RC Car Racing Facility** has officially opened near Moonlight Festival Park in Songdo, Incheon! ### 1. Facility Overview & Parking

* **Parking:** Free on-site parking accommodating up to 40 vehicles and 2 buses. Overflow public parking is available nearby near the futsal and baseball fields.
* **Trial Period:** Operating on a trial basis through the end of August 2026.
* **Hours:** 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM (Closed on Mondays).

​

---

### 2. Circuit Layouts (3 Dedicated Tracks)

​

#### Blue Track (Main Circuit)
* **Scale:** Designed primarily for 1:10 scale touring and on-road RC cars.
* **Features:** Includes a raised air-conditioned driver control cabin allowing pilots to command their cars comfortably during hot summer months.

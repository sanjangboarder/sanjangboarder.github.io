As autumn deepens, many anglers begin looking into schedules for Mokpo cutlassfish boat fishing. Mokpo is widely recognized as one of the best destinations for first-time boat anglers because the transit by boat to fishing spots is very short, and everything from booking to boarding is straightforward and accessible. In this post, I will provide an easy-to-follow guide for Mokpo cutlassfish beginners, covering everything from essential preparation to a typical day's schedule.

​

> **When Should You Go for Mokpo Cutlassfish Fishing?**

​

The cutlassfish season runs from early August through late November. Early in the season, you can enjoy catching high quantities (numbers), while the mid-to-late season brings opportunities for thick, larger-grade cutlassfish, so each period offers its own distinct charm. Because fishing conditions are heavily influenced by tides and weather, it is best to check the latest catch reports with the charter company before finalizing your dates. Weekend charters book out quickly, so once you settle on a date, booking promptly is recommended.

> **Which Boat Should You Book?**

​

The departure port is located at Pier 3, Mokpo North Port (11, Goha-daero 597beon-gil, Mokpo-si). The port is easily accessible via a quick taxi ride from Mokpo Station, making it completely stress-free even for travelers using public transit. Operating out of this pier, the Mokpo Seohae Gold Ho is a 9.77-ton, 22-passenger vessel designed with spacious deck passageways and generous spacing between angling stations. This minimizes the risk of line tangles with neighboring anglers and allows you to enjoy cutlassfish fishing in clean, comfortable surroundings.

> **Pre-Trip Checklist for Mokpo Cutlassfish Boat Fishing**

​

Packing for Mokpo cutlassfish boat fishing is delightfully simple: a government-issued photo ID (mandatory for boarding), warm outer layers to withstand chilly night sea breezes, and motion sickness medication if you are prone to seasickness. Because cutlassfish possess razor-sharp teeth, bringing a pair of cotton work gloves ensures much safer fish handling.

​

On the Mokpo North Port Seohae Gold Ho, the boarding fee of 80,000 KRW includes complimentary rental of two complete rod-and-reel setups, free terminal tackle rigs, and unlimited fresh bait, so you can arrive empty-handed without worrying about specialized gear. In addition, hot ramen is served free of charge whenever you feel hungry out on the water.

> **Mokpo Cutlassfish Fishing Schedule Preview**

​

Inner-bay cutlassfish charters out of Mokpo depart at 6:00 PM and return to port between 2:00 AM and 3:00 AM. Reaching prime fishing points takes only about 30 minutes, removing any long transit fatigue, and because the boat remains within sheltered coastal waters, even complete novices can fish comfortably without rough ocean swells. Sailing out toward the captain's secret spots also offers scenic views of Mokpo's illuminated coastline at night. Once on station, the most critical window begins: at dusk, as the powerful fish-attracting lights (attraction lamps) switch on, schools of cutlassfish swarm beneath the glow, making the period right after sunset the most explosive bite window of the night.

> **Tips to Truly Experience the Thrill of the Cutlassfish Bite**

​

Even beginners can dramatically boost their catch count by keeping one vital rule in mind: finding the exact depth stratum where cutlassfish are actively suspended. The captain continually broadcasts the target water depth over the loudspeaker at each spot, so all you have to do is drop your rig directly to that specified depth level.

When you feel distinct tapping bites followed by a heavy bend loaded onto the rod tip, begin reeling up slowly and steadily. The angling technique is simple and forgiving, so first-timers pick it up almost immediately. The excitement of reeling in with anticipation as a brilliant silver cutlassfish flashes and glints beneath the surface lights is an unmatched thrill unique to Mokpo cutlassfish night fishing.

> **A Unique Delicacy Savored Only Onboard**

​

If I had to pick the single greatest highlight of Mokpo cutlassfish boat fishing, it would undoubtedly be tasting freshly caught cutlassfish sashimi (eun-galchi hoe) right on the boat deck. This is an unmissable culinary delight. The rich, nutty, and delicate melt-in-your-mouth flavor of freshly sliced silver cutlassfish is an extraordinary experience virtually impossible to replicate on land. Sharing this delicacy with fellow anglers during a relaxing break washes away overnight fatigue and creates unforgettable memories.

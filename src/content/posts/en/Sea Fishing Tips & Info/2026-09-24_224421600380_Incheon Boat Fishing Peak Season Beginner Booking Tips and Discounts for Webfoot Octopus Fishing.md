Incheon Namhang Recreational Fishing Pier is the largest hub in Korea with a dense concentration of fishing fleets tailored perfectly for beginners. Usually, when people mention webfoot octopus and cuttlefish fishing, they refer to 9.77-ton 22-passenger high-speed charter boats. But that involves an exhausting schedule where you arrive around 3 AM and don't finish until 4 to 5 PM—a forced march that makes beginners wonder why they are even doing it.

Once you have booked your trip, let me also highlight some invaluable practical tips that will make your experience smooth and enjoyable.

Most charter fleets offer tackle rental, so if it's your first time, there is no need to purchase expensive gear upfront—just bring a small ice cooler. Tackle rental fees are typically around 10,000 KRW, and basic rigs and terminal tackle can be purchased cheaply on-site from the charter office.

![](https://mblogthumb-phinf.pstatic.net/MjAyNjA5MjRfMjYz/MDAxNzkwMjA5NDMxMzA4.kBjhiYT2rO_IeEvG8liT2Bh7c52aajoB8Rpu46FAXfog.4I1xOkNUH6B85NQsNE61f1Oupb2rCzMXMp2wPnLHwxwg.PNG/image.png?type=w800)

Regarding parking: For hourly half-day boats, aisle parking inside Namhang is permitted. The gate is usually opened between 5:00 AM and 5:20 AM. If you wait in line near the Namhang entrance gate around that time, parking is relatively smooth. Please note that the maximum queue length that will still manage to get inside extends back to the intersection leading into Yeonan Pier Market. If you arrive late and it looks like the main parking lot will fill up, parking at a nearby public parking lot is your best bet. Also, roadside parking is subject to enforcement on weekdays but generally not enforced on weekends, so roadside parking on weekends is an option. However, never park in prohibited areas such as pedestrian crossings, fire hydrants, or road curves, as you can still be ticketed there even on weekends.

Hello, this is Sanjangboarder.

As the current webfoot octopus and cuttlefish season approaches its closing stages, I have already started contemplating plans for next year's season. The widely consulted tide tables on Badatime typically do not open until January 1st of the upcoming year. Consequently, planning early reservations—especially competitive boat bookings for autumn octopus—has always involved tedious rough estimations. Finding this process inconvenient, I took the initiative to build a custom solution that visualizes key regional tide forecasts directly on Google Calendar.

Focusing on major hub ports along the West Coast where I primarily fish, I also integrated night coastal foraging (haerujil) tides into the system. Rather than displaying raw, convoluted data tables, the calendar is intuitively styled to highlight core metrics at a single glance.

**📱 1. Fast 3-Second Subscription via Google Calendar**

By registering the URL feed in Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, an entire year's worth of tides and traffic light indices syncs automatically across all your devices.

1. Open Google Calendar (on PC or mobile web browser).
2. Click the '+' button next to "Other calendars" on the left sidebar.
3. Select the [From URL] menu option.
4. Copy and paste the ICS webcal link for your preferred port to finish.

💡 The service currently supports 9 major strategic ports: Incheon, Pyeongtaek, Sinjindo (Anheung), Mageompo (Taean), Wonsando (Boryeong), Muchangpo (Boryeong), Hongwon Port (Seocheon), Gunsan, and Mokpo.

**🔍 3. Detailed Field Guidelines & Safety Protocols upon Event Click**

Tapping into any schedule item provides actionable tactical guidance suitable even for complete novices.

◦ Target Species Suitability: Star ratings for webfoot octopus, cuttlefish, flounder, and rockfish
◦ Low & High Tide Times: Exact timestamps for lowest water (low tide) and peak refill (high tide)

​

🚨 Foraging Water Exit Safety Rules:
🚪 Recommended Entry: Begin wading 2 hours prior to lowest tide (low water)
🚨 Mandatory Exit: Must exit the flat within 1 hour after low tide! (Once the tide reverses, water rushes back rapidly with extreme hazards)
⚠️ Safety Protocols: Always partner up in pairs, wear life vests, and set redundant smartphone exit alarms

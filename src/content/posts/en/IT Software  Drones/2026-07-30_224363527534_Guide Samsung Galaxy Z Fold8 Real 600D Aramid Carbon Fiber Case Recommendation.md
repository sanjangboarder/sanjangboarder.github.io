### 1. Understanding Aramid Weave Densities (600D vs 1000D vs 1500D)

Not all carbon fiber cases are created equal. Aramid fiber is categorized by thread density (Denier):

​

| Specification | 600D (Ultra-Fine Premium) | 1000D (Balanced) | 1500D (Rough & Heavy) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yarn Thickness** | Finest & Most Precise | Medium Thickness | Thickest & Heavy |
| **Weave Pattern** | Ultra-Fine & Tight Grid | Medium Pattern | Large Coarse Pattern |
| **Tactile Texture** | Soft Matte Precision | Standard Grip | Rough Textured |
| **Weight / Slimness** | Lightest & Thinnest | Medium | Heaviest |
| **Price Point** | Premium Line | Mid-Tier | Budget Line |

​

For foldable devices, **600D aramid fiber** is the gold standard because it provides rigid scratch/drop protection while keeping the phone's slim profile intact.

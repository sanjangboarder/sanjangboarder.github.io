**Why I Upgraded to the Galaxy Z Fold 8**

As a developer, a few key technical upgrades drove my decision to purchase:
1. **Aspect Ratio Redesign:** The cover screen aspect ratio has been widened to feel like a standard smartphone when closed, solving the key usability complaint of previous narrow Fold screens.
2. **Carbon-Silicon Battery Technology:** Samsung incorporated high-density carbon-silicon battery cells, achieving a slimmer profile while increasing battery capacity.
3. **Slim & Lightweight Ergonomics:** Substantial weight reduction makes one-handed holding far more comfortable.

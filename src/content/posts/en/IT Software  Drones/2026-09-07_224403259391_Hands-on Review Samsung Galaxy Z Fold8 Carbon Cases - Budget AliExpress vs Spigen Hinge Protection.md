### Conclusion & Buying Guide

The Spigen case is approximately 4 times the price of the AliExpress carbon case. Is it worth 4x? If Spigen’s hinge shield prevents a single catastrophic drop from damaging the internal folding screen, it pays for itself ten times over.

* **For Samsung Care+ / Device Insurance Subscribers:** The slim, featherweight AliExpress carbon case is a great choice since screen replacement deductibles are low.
* **Without Insurance:** A damaged hinge or inner display easily costs upwards of 800,000 KRW out of pocket. In this case, Spigen's Hinge Protection Aramid case is by far the smarter financial investment.

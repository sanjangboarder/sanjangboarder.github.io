Next to the restroom is an **air compressor system** for dusting off gear. It looks extremely useful for quickly blowing away dust after driving RC cars hard on the track. Since it is an asphalt track, dust won't be excessive, but paying attention to amenities like this is great to see.

**Auxiliary Track**

Next is the auxiliary track circuit... It features fewer long straightaways compared to the main track, making it a course that requires more precise cornering control. As a complete beginner, I think I'll start slow on this track to get a feel for control.

​

​

The auxiliary track control booth is virtually identical to the main track building. Equipped with air conditioning and large glass windows, it offers a clear view overlooking the entire circuit. If popularity grows, driving here will be a fun experience.

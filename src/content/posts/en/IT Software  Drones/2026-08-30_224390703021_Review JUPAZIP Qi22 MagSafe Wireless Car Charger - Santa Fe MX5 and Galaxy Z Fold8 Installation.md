### 1. Mounting Location & Santa Fe MX5 Cockpit Integration

Rather than clipping the mount into air vents, I attached it to a dedicated head unit screen mount bracket on the Santa Fe MX5. Mounting it on the far right edge of the screen minimizes obstruction while keeping driving vision completely clear.

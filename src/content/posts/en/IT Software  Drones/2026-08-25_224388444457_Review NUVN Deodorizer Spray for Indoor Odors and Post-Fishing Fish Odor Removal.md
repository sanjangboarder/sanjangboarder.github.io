Instead of spraying into thin air, spray NUVN directly and thoroughly inside fishing coolers and tackle containers. Using a **45.15Hz ultra-low cluster deodorizing particle technology**, it directly breaks down, separates, and coats odor molecules before evaporating, neutralizing stubborn fish odors instantly.
